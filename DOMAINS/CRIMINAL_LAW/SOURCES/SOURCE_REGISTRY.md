# Hình Sự (Criminal Law & True Crime) Source Registry

Status: active foundation (upgraded from `planned` 2026-07-22). Tier system follows `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3's Source Priority Hierarchy. This registry is stricter than the FS/BUD domains' by necessity: this domain's factual claims can name real, sometimes-living people, so source discipline here is a defamation/harm control, not just a quality control.

| Source tier | Allowed source type | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official primary records — court verdicts (bản án), indictments (cáo trạng), government statutes (Bộ luật Hình sự, Bộ luật Tố tụng Hình sự), official law-enforcement statements | active | Highest confidence; required for any statement of a final conviction or a specific legal-status claim (§4). |
| 2 | Established investigative journalism — named, reputable outlets with documented editorial standards; named journalists/authors with a track record on the specific case | active | Required for any claim about an unsolved case's suspects/theories (§5) and for organized-crime designations (§4a Format 3). |
| 3 | Academic criminology, legal scholarship, peer-reviewed forensic/psychological literature | active | Used for systemic, procedural, and psychological-pattern claims — never as the sole source for an individual's guilt. |
| 4 | Documented true-crime books/long-form documentaries with citations | restricted | Usable only after cross-checking against tiers 1-3; narrative flourishes in these sources are not themselves citable facts. |
| 5 | Popular secondary compilations (Wikipedia-tier, crime wikis, aggregator articles) | restricted | Narrative color and cross-check only — never the sole source for an identity claim, a guilt claim, or a victim-identifying detail. |
| 6 | Modern reflective/applied commentary produced in-house (systemic-issue framing, §10's societal-reflective layer) | restricted | Must be labeled as the channel's own reflective framing, not as a sourced factual claim — mirrors Tier 5/6 in the FS/BUD registries. |

**Explicitly forbidden as sources (not merely low-tier — do not use at all):** anonymous forums (Reddit-tier speculation, imageboards), unverified social-media claims, doxxing/stalking sites, citation-free "conspiracy" true-crime channels, and any source whose primary content is unmasking a private individual's identity or current location.

Do not invent a case, a name, a verdict, a statute number, or a quote. Every Knowledge Packet built for this domain must cite its sources per case/claim, cross-referenced against at least two independent tier-1-to-3 sources before a claim is treated as settled (per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3 and §5).
