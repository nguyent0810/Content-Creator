# Hình Sự (Criminal Law & True Crime) Glossary

Each entry gives the term, a working definition, and the editorial rule/risk flag governing its use, mirroring how `DOMAINS/FENG_SHUI/GLOSSARY/DOMAIN_GLOSSARY.md` and `DOMAINS/BUDDHISM/GLOSSARY/DOMAIN_GLOSSARY.md` are structured. ## A. Legal-Status Terms (the domain's single most safety-critical vocabulary — see `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4)

**Bị tình nghi (suspected)** — A person law enforcement suspects but has not formally charged. *Rule:* the only correct term before formal charges exist; never upgrade to "bị can"/"bị cáo" language before that status actually applies.

**Bị can** — A person formally charged and under active criminal investigation (post-khởi tố, pre-trial). *Rule:* still presumed innocent; never described as having "done" the crime, only as charged with it.

**Bị cáo** — A person charged and standing trial (post-truy tố, at xét xử stage). *Rule:* same presumption-of-innocence requirement as bị can; this is a procedural-stage label, not a guilt label.

**Bị hại (victim/injured party)** — The person(s) harmed by the alleged offense. *Rule:* subject to the full privacy/dignity protections in `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §6, especially for minors and sexual-violence cases.

**Người bị buộc tội (person accused)** — The umbrella term (Điều 4, Bộ luật Tố tụng Hình sự 2015) covering everyone from a person just arrested (người bị bắt) through người bị tạm giữ, bị can, and bị cáo. *Rule:* same presumption-of-innocence requirement as its narrower sub-terms; used when explaining rights/principles that apply across all these stages at once (e.g., nguyên tắc suy đoán vô tội, quyền bào chữa) — added per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §11's extension workflow when `KNOWLEDGE_PACKETS/KP_CL_001_Luat_Hinh_Su.md` made this term foundational to Pillar 1.

**Bản án có hiệu lực pháp luật (a verdict with legal effect)** — A verdict that is final and non-appealable (appeal window expired, or highest appeal decided). *Rule:* this is the precise threshold at which a person may be named and described factually as having committed the crime (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4) — a first-instance verdict alone (see "xét xử sơ thẩm" below) does not meet this threshold if still appealable.

**Kháng cáo** — An appeal filed by the defendant or another party against a verdict. **Kháng nghị** — An appeal/protest filed by the Viện Kiểm Sát (prosecution) or a court on its own authority. *Rule:* if either is pending at time of publication, content must disclose the case is not yet final, even if an earlier verdict already named a defendant.

**Giám đốc thẩm / Tái thẩm** — Extraordinary review procedures (supervisory review / retrial based on newly discovered facts) that can reopen even an already-final verdict. *Rule:* rare but must be checked for high-profile cases before treating a verdict as permanently settled.

## B. Procedural & Institutional Terms

**Khởi tố (initiate criminal proceedings)** — The formal start of a criminal investigation against a person or case.

**Truy tố (prosecute/indict)** — The formal charging decision that moves a case from investigation to trial, issued via a cáo trạng (indictment).

**Xét xử sơ thẩm (first-instance trial)** / **Xét xử phúc thẩm (appellate trial)** — Vietnam's two-tier trial structure. *Rule:* a sơ thẩm verdict alone is not yet "bản án có hiệu lực pháp luật" if the appeal window remains open — see above.

**Cơ quan Điều tra (CQĐT, Investigation Agency)**, **Viện Kiểm sát Nhân dân (VKSND, People's Procuracy)**, **Tòa án Nhân dân (TAND, People's Court)** — the three core institutions moving a Vietnamese criminal case from investigation → prosecution decision → trial.

**Án treo (suspended sentence)**, **Án tử hình (death penalty)** — sentencing outcomes; state plainly and factually once part of a final verdict, without editorializing on the sentence's justice or injustice (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §10's societal-reflective layer is the correct place for systemic sentencing critique, clearly labeled as such).

**Tình tiết tăng nặng / giảm nhẹ (aggravating / mitigating circumstances)** — Factors a Vietnamese court weighs in sentencing; useful for explaining *why* a sentence was what it was, factually, from the verdict record.

## C. True-Crime / Cross-Jurisdiction Terms

**Nghi phạm (suspect) / Người liên quan (person of interest)** — Terms for Format 2 content (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4a) in unsolved cases; always paired with a named tier-1-to-3 source and theory-labeling language, never stated as a conclusion.

**Tổ chức tội phạm (criminal organization)** — Umbrella term for mafia, hội Tam Hoàng (Triads), cartels, and comparable syndicates (Pillar 5). *Rule:* the organization's documented designation must itself trace to a tier-1/tier-2 source; naming the organization never licenses naming an individual unconvicted member as guilty (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4a Format 3).

**Hội Tam Hoàng (Triads)** — Chinese-origin organized-crime societies with a documented multi-century history; treat historically/sociologically per §8, never as an aspirational lifestyle narrative.

**Common law vs. Civil law** — The two broad legal-system families; Vietnam is civil-law (codified statute, no binding precedent in the common-law sense), while jurisdictions like the U.S./U.K. are common-law (precedent-based, jury trial). *Rule:* never assume a foreign case's procedural concept (grand jury, plea bargain, Miranda-style rights) applies in a Vietnamese-law context, or vice versa (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2).
