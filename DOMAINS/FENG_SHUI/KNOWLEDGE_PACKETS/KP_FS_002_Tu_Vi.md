# Executive Summary

Tử Vi Đẩu Số is a Chinese-origin birth-chart astrological system that maps a person's lunar birth year, month, day, and hour onto a wheel of 12 fixed houses ("Cung"), each populated by stars ("sao") drawn primarily from a core group of 14 "chính tinh" (major stars) plus numerous "phụ tinh" (minor stars). It shares its deep grammar — Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Thiên Can, Địa Chi — with the broader Sino-Vietnamese calendrical and divinatory toolkit also used in Phong Thủy, but Tử Vi's specific application of that grammar (chart construction, star placement, house reading) is a distinct discipline covered by this packet.

The system's popular founding narrative credits the Song-dynasty Daoist figure Trần Đoàn (Hy Di), but this attribution is traditional lore, not verified biographical history — the term "Tử Vi Đẩu Số" itself is not attested in writing until 1581, several centuries after Trần Đoàn's traditional dates, and even the Chinese textual tradition preserves a competing variant crediting Lã Động Tân. Vietnamese Tử Vi has its own accumulated commentary layer, most visibly through the mid-20th-century Quốc Ngữ textual tradition (Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang) and the "Nghiệm Lý" school associated with cụ Thiên Lương — both real, cross-confirmed lineages as schools/traditions. A 2026-07-24 follow-up pass narrowed the two founders' biographical gaps differently: cụ Thiên Lương's real name and birth/death dates (Lê Quang Khải, 1910-1985) are now medium-high confidence, with an explicit caveat that the corroborating sources may not be fully independent of one another; Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang's biography remains a genuinely unresolved gap even after the follow-up pass, including a re-confirmed community-wide open question about whether he is still alive.

This packet documents: (1) the historical layering of Tử Vi's origins, tiered by confidence exactly as `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §7-8 tiers Kinh Địa Tạng's provenance questions; (2) the structural building blocks — Thiên Can, Địa Chi, the general chart-construction process, and the 12 Cung; (3) the 14 Chính Tinh, their traditional natures, and their Ngũ Hành associations, flagging the substantial cross-source disagreement on five of the fourteen stars' elemental attribution; and (4) domain-specific editorial rules, with special emphasis on the two rules most likely to be violated by an inexperienced scriptwriter: the ban on individualized readings (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4) and the ban on any specific lifespan or major-misfortune timing claim (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §8).

This packet is structural and historical only. It does not contain, and must never be extended into, personal chart-reading instructions, star-combination "verdicts," or any content inviting a viewer to calculate and self-apply their own chart.

# Historical Background

Following the three-tier confidence framework already established for Kinh Địa Tạng (`BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §7-8: historical / traditional attribution / unverified-legendary) and required by `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2's instruction to attribute the Trần Đoàn story as "popular/traditional attribution, not verified biographical history."

## Tầng 1 — Relatively well-attested (multiple independent sources, Vietnamese and Chinese/English, in agreement)

- Tử Vi Đẩu Số took recognizable shape during the Song dynasty (960–1279), synthesizing Daoist cosmology, classical Chinese astronomical observation, and Âm Dương–Ngũ Hành numerology. Both English-language sources (Wikipedia "Zi wei dou shu," StarBook) and Chinese-language sources (Epoch Times Chinese edition, KKnews) agree on this general Song-era formation window.
- The earliest known written appearance of the exact term "Tử Vi Đẩu Số" is in the text *Tử Vi Đẩu Số Tiệp Lãm*, compiled in the 9th year of the Ming Wanli era (1581) — meaning the earliest textual attestation of the name postdates Trần Đoàn's traditional lifetime (10th century) by several centuries. This gap is an important piece of context whenever the Trần Đoàn attribution is mentioned.
- The text most schools treat as foundational, *Tử Vi Đẩu Số Toàn Thư*, survives through an edition compiled/edited by La Hồng Tiên (a Ming Jiajing-era jinshi degree holder); it is that edition's preface — a later, retrospective document — that attributes the original work to Hy Di Trần Đoàn. The attribution therefore originates from a posthumous preface, not a contemporaneous record of Trần Đoàn's own authorship.

## Tầng 2 — Popular/traditional attribution, NOT independently verified as biography

- Trần Đoàn (陳摶, c. 871–989 CE), Daoist name Hy Di, also known as Phù Diêu Tử, is a historically real Daoist figure (recorded as a hermit on Mount Hoa Sơn and discussed in broader histories of Daoist thought and Kinh Dịch scholarship). **His specific role as "founder/systematizer of Tử Vi Đẩu Số" is a traditional attribution, not a verified historical event.** The English Wikipedia article states plainly that the attribution to Trần Đoàn "cannot be verified historically" and describes him as a "semi-legendary figure" in this context.
- Numerous Vietnamese-language sources recount a fuller legend: that Trần Đoàn studied six fields (astronomy, calendrics, Ngũ Hành, "ngũ sự," miscellaneous divination, and physiognomy) and composed a work titled "Tử Vi Tinh Nghĩa" presented to Song Emperor Taizu. This is a **later, retrospective legendary detail**, with no contemporary (10th-century) source confirming it — it should always be introduced with framing language such as "tương truyền" (it is traditionally said) or "theo truyền thuyết" (according to legend), never as settled biography.
- A competing Chinese-language variant credits Lã Động Tân (Lữ Đồng Tân), one of the Eight Immortals, as the one who transmitted the system to Trần Đoàn before Trần Đoàn's own attainment of the Way. The existence of this rival version, inside the Chinese tradition itself, is direct evidence that the "founding story" is a plural, competing set of legends rather than one settled historical account — this point (the plurality itself) is safe, well-sourced, and useful content.
- A further, weaker claim (found in a single popular Chinese-language source, Storm Media, quoting an unnamed "mệnh lý gia") suggests Tử Vi Đẩu Số's deeper roots trace to "Thất Chính Tứ Dư," an astrological system said to have entered China via Buddhist monks and Arab traders during the Tang dynasty. This claim is **single-sourced in the current research pass and not cross-checked** — it must not be presented as an established fact, only (if used at all) as "một giả thuyết chưa được kiểm chứng."

## Tầng 3 — Introduction and development in Vietnam

- A widely repeated story on Vietnamese websites (Kabala, trithucvn2.net, and a small cluster of related pages) holds that in 1257 (year Đinh Tỵ), a Song-dynasty official named Hoàng Bính, skilled in astronomy/Tử Vi/physiognomy, fled to Đại Việt with his family (some versions say roughly 2,000 people) under Trần Thái Tông, bringing "Tử Vi Tinh Nghĩa" and "Triệu Thị Minh Thuyết Tử Vi Kinh," and thereby transmitted Tử Vi into the Trần court (associated with names such as Trần Quang Khải, Trần Nhật Duật, Trần Quốc Tảng). **This story is currently attested only by a small cluster of mutually similar popular websites and was not confirmed against independent historiographical sources (official Trần-dynasty chronicles, academic history) in this research pass.** It must be treated strictly as internet-circulated legend/lore, never presented as a confirmed historical event, and always framed with language such as "theo một giai thoại được lưu truyền" (according to a circulating legend).
- What can be said with more confidence: Tử Vi Đẩu Số entered Vietnam from China through a long process (no precise date can currently be confirmed), likely via multiple channels of Sino-Vietnamese cultural exchange across several dynasties, and developed into its own distinct scholarly/practice tradition in Vietnam, with especially visible activity in 20th-century Hà Nội and later Sài Gòn.
- **Vietnamese authors/schools with confirmed cross-source influence:**
  - **Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang** — author of *Tử Vi Đẩu Số Tân Biên* (published in Saigon, c. 1955–1956). Multiple independent sources (quansachmuathu, tuvi.cohoc.net, lyso.vn, sachbaokhang) agree this is one of the most influential Quốc Ngữ Tử Vi texts, credited with popularizing the system among young Southern intellectuals in the mid-20th century, and it remains one of the most reprinted/circulated foundational texts today. One source claims the author was formerly a Buddhist Đại Đức (senior monk) — this detail appears in only one source in this research pass and is **not cross-verified**; do not state it as fact without further confirmation. **This gap remains genuinely unresolved as of the 2026-07-24 follow-up pass** — see the dedicated "Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang" note under Risk-Specific/Historical QA below for the re-confirmed status and why this is a real open question, not a research shortfall.
  - **Cụ Thiên Lương** — real name, birth, and death dates **researched further on 2026-07-24** (`SOURCES/RESEARCH_DRAFT_GAP_CLOSURE_2026_07.md` §3): **Lê Quang Khải**, born 12/07/1910 (dương lịch; 06/06 âm lịch, giờ Mão) in làng Phượng Lau, Hưng Yên, died 17/09/1985 (22/08 âm lịch), aged 76. This name and these dates appeared identically across every source checked in that pass (dangxuanxuyen blog via search snippet, hoctuvi.blogspot.com full-text, lyso.vn forum full-text). **The earlier variant "Lê Quang Vinh" was not found in any source consulted in the 2026-07-24 pass and should be dropped** unless a source using that name is located later. **Important independence caveat — do not overstate this as fully cross-verified:** the wording describing his biography is near-identical across the sites that repeat it (dangxuanxuyen, hoctuvi.blogspot.com, lyso.vn, sachhoc.com, metaisach.com, sonchu.vn, quansachmuathu.vn), strongly suggesting they all trace back to a single original biographical article (likely the original "Khoa Học Huyền Bí" magazine profile by Trần Việt Sơn, or Đặng Xuân Xuyến's compiled piece) reposted across many domains — **not several genuinely independent research sources converging**. Confidence for the name and dates is therefore **medium-high** (consistent, no conflicting version found anywhere, stable across years of reposting), **not high** in the full sense of `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3's "2-3 independent sources" bar — a source with a genuinely different editorial origin (e.g., a digitized original "Khoa Học Huyền Bí" issue, or a mainstream press piece) is still needed before calling this fully cross-verified. Family/career detail confirmed in the same pass: father was a locally known geomancer; elder brother was a noted Kinh Dịch commentator in 1950s-60s Saigon under the pen name "Đẩu Sơn"; joined anti-French activity with the VNQDĐ at 18; later worked at the "Tổng Nha Ngân Sách và Ngoại Viện" 1954-1971; the pen name "Thiên Lương" only came into public use around 1970. His son (Lê Hưng) continued the school post-1975 as "Hậu Thiên Lương" (already established in this packet, re-confirmed by the new pass).
  - Other schools/authors are mentioned in sources (e.g., "Tử Vi Nam Phái" attributed to Lê Quang Lăng) but current research depth is insufficient to confirm their relative influence — do not list them as major lineages until further sourced.

## Historical Debates (production rule)

The central debate is provenance: whether Trần Đoàn is a real founder, a retrospective figurehead attached to a later compiled tradition, or one of several competing legendary originators (cf. the Lã Động Tân variant). Content must not resolve this debate one way or the other. The correct framing, mirroring the Kinh Địa Tạng packet's approach to its own provenance debate:

- Tử Vi Đẩu Số is a revered, centuries-old East Asian astrological tradition with a rich, textually attested Ming-era canon (from 1581 onward) and a widely repeated — but not historically verified — attribution to the Song-era figure Trần Đoàn.
- The Trần Đoàn story, the Lã Động Tân variant, and the Hoàng Bính 1257 transmission-to-Vietnam story are all traditional narratives with strong cultural currency and weak-to-absent independent documentary confirmation as literal history.
- The tradition's cultural and structural significance in Vietnam does not depend on any of these founding legends being literally true.

# Structural Foundation

## Âm Dương trong Tử Vi

**Knowledge function:** Âm Dương supplies Tử Vi's most basic classificatory axis — used to sort star nature (active/expansive vs. still/inward) and, in some practitioner traditions, to assess the "fit" between a chart's Mệnh and its Cục.

**Primary concepts:**
- Nam Đẩu star group (e.g., Thiên Phủ, Thất Sát) commonly framed as Dương — active, outward-facing.
- Bắc Đẩu star group (e.g., Tử Vi, Liêm Trinh) commonly framed as Âm — inward, contemplative. (Note: this Âm/Dương framing of the two groups is distinct from, and should not be confused with, the group *names* "Bắc Đẩu"/"Nam Đẩu" themselves, which are directional/asterism-based naming conventions — see the 14 Chính Tinh section below.)
- Thái Dương and Thái Âm — the two stars representing the sun and moon directly — are the clearest embodiment of the Âm/Dương polarity within the star system.
- "Âm Dương thuận lý / nghịch lý" — a concept, used by some practitioners, correlating a chart's gender (Nam/Nữ mệnh) with whether Cung Mệnh falls on an Âm or Dương Địa Chi position, used as one indicator of a chart's "naturalness."

**Production caution:** This entire application layer is **Tier 3 (contemporary popular practice)**, not cross-school consensus. Always frame with "theo một số cách luận truyền thống" (according to some traditional interpretive approaches) rather than presenting a single fixed rule, per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3 and §5.

## Ngũ Hành trong Tử Vi — "Cục" (Ngũ Hành Cục)

**Knowledge function:** Every completed lá số is assigned to one of five "Cục," each tied to an element and a number used in star placement: Thủy Nhị Cục (2), Mộc Tam Cục (3), Kim Tứ Cục (4), Thổ Ngũ Cục (5), Hỏa Lục Cục (6).

**Primary concepts:**
- Cục is treated by many practitioner sources as the "environment/context" surrounding Cung Mệnh.
- Practitioners commonly describe reading the Sinh/Khắc relationship between the Cục's element and the chart's own "bản Mệnh" element (itself derived via Nạp Âm from the birth year) as part of traditional interpretation — Sinh (generating) relationships traditionally read as more favorable, Khắc (overcoming) relationships as more challenging.
- **The precise historical/technical reason the numbers 2-3-4-5-6 attach to each specific element is not settled in the sources consulted** — this is a genuinely open, debated technical point among Tử Vi researchers, not a case of the channel simply not having found the answer yet. Do not present a single "explanation" for the numbering as definitive.

**Production caution:** Cục mechanics are low-confidence/still-debated technical detail (per source: "chi tiết kỹ thuật thấp độ tin cậy"). Any script content touching Cục should stay at the conceptual level (what a Cục is, that it exists) rather than asserting a specific numerological justification as fact.

## Thiên Can (10 Heavenly Stems)

**Knowledge function:** A 10-symbol cycle used, together with Địa Chi, to name days and years in the East Asian lunar calendar system (10 Can × 12 Chi, reduced by an odd/even pairing rule, yields the 60-term Lục Thập Hoa Giáp cycle). Each Can carries one Ngũ Hành element and one Âm/Dương polarity. Thiên Can is shared infrastructure used across Tử Vi, Bát Tự/Tứ Trụ, the lunar calendar, and Phong Thủy — not unique to Tử Vi.

**Primary concepts / table (high confidence — cross-verified across 3 independent sources with no disagreement):**

| # | Thiên Can | Âm/Dương | Ngũ Hành |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Giáp | Dương | Mộc |
| 2 | Ất | Âm | Mộc |
| 3 | Bính | Dương | Hỏa |
| 4 | Đinh | Âm | Hỏa |
| 5 | Mậu | Dương | Thổ |
| 6 | Kỷ | Âm | Thổ |
| 7 | Canh | Dương | Kim |
| 8 | Tân | Âm | Kim |
| 9 | Nhâm | Dương | Thủy |
| 10 | Quý | Âm | Thủy |

Memory pattern: adjacent pairs share an element (Giáp-Ất/Mộc, Bính-Đinh/Hỏa, Mậu-Kỷ/Thổ, Canh-Tân/Kim, Nhâm-Quý/Thủy); within each pair, the first is Dương, the second Âm.

**Narrative detail:** Popular sources generally attribute the invention of Can-Chi to the legendary figure Đại Nạo (Đại Sào) in Chinese lore; separately, some Vietnamese linguists (e.g., Nguyễn Cung Thông) have proposed a Việt-Mường etymological connection for the 12-animal names. **This is an unsettled academic area** — content must not assert a single-nation origin as proven fact; present as "truyền thuyết phổ biến" and "một giả thuyết học thuật" respectively.

**Script-ready material:** Can as the "sky-layer" name riding atop the Chi cycle is a usable visual metaphor ("lớp áo trời phủ lên chu kỳ Chi") for explaining why lunar years have two-character names (e.g., "năm Giáp Tý") — always illustrate with a *generic* example year, never a viewer's actual birth year.

**Production cautions:**
- Never suggest a birth-year Can determines an individualized fate outcome — this is structural/calendrical data, not a personalized reading (§4).
- Never present the origin debate as settled in favor of one nation/ethnicity.
- Never build a segment listing "your Can" tied to a specific birth-date lookup invitation.

## Địa Chi (12 Earthly Branches)

**Knowledge function:** A 12-symbol cycle running parallel to Thiên Can, used for hours of the day, months, and the 12-animal year cycle. In Tử Vi specifically, Địa Chi has a dual role: (1) it encodes birth hour, a mandatory chart-construction input; (2) it names the 12 fixed positions on the "địa bàn" wheel where the 12 Cung are placed.

**Primary concepts / table (high confidence — cross-verified across 3 independent sources):**

| # | Địa Chi | Con giáp | Khung giờ | Ngũ Hành |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tý | Chuột | 23h–1h | Thủy |
| 2 | Sửu | Trâu | 1h–3h | Thổ |
| 3 | Dần | Hổ | 3h–5h | Mộc |
| 4 | Mão | Mèo (VN) / Thỏ (TQ) | 5h–7h | Mộc |
| 5 | Thìn | Rồng | 7h–9h | Thổ |
| 6 | Tỵ | Rắn | 9h–11h | Hỏa |
| 7 | Ngọ | Ngựa | 11h–13h | Hỏa |
| 8 | Mùi | Dê | 13h–15h | Thổ |
| 9 | Thân | Khỉ | 15h–17h | Kim |
| 10 | Dậu | Gà | 17h–19h | Kim |
| 11 | Tuất | Chó | 19h–21h | Thổ |
| 12 | Hợi | Lợn | 21h–23h | Thủy |

Note: unlike Thiên Can (2 branches per element), Thổ occupies 4 Địa Chi positions (Sửu, Thìn, Mùi, Tuất — tied to the four seasonal-transition points), while Mộc, Hỏa, Kim, Thủy each occupy 2.

**Confirmed cultural variant (not an error to "correct"):** the 4th animal is "Mèo" (cat) in Vietnamese tradition versus "Thỏ" (rabbit) in Chinese tradition — a well-documented, genuine cultural divergence, safe and interesting production material in its own right.

**Script-ready material:** explaining "giờ Tý" and why Tử Vi requires a precise birth hour is safe educational content, provided no invitation to submit a real birth hour is made. The Mèo/Thỏ divergence is strong, low-risk material for a segment on Vietnamese cultural distinctiveness within a shared regional system.

**Production cautions:**
- Never use the hour table to invite viewers to "calculate your hour and comment what you are" — this crosses into personalized reading invitation (§4).
- Do not assert a single-nation origin of the 12-animal cycle as proven history; Nguyễn Cung Thông's Việt-Mường hypothesis is one hypothesis among others, not academic consensus.

## Cách lập lá số Tử Vi cơ bản (concept only — not a how-to)

**Knowledge function:** Tử Vi uses four birth data points — lunar year, month, day, hour — plus gender, to construct a 12-house chart. General process: (1) locate Cung Mệnh using month and hour via a counting procedure on the Địa Chi wheel; (2) "an sao" — place chính tinh and phụ tinh into the 12 Cung using year (Can-Chi), month, and day, per each school's fixed placement rules. Detailed star-placement mechanics are explicitly out of scope for this packet (reserved for a future, dedicated packet) and were not researched in sufficient depth to state as settled technique here.

**Primary concepts:**

| Input | Role |
|---|---|
| Năm sinh (lunar, Can-Chi) | Determines birth-year Can-Chi; used to place certain chính tinh and year-based fate-cycle stars |
| Tháng sinh (lunar) | Used in the provisional-position step of locating Cung Mệnh; also used to place certain month-based stars |
| Ngày sinh (lunar) | Used to place the Tử Vi star and its associated chính tinh chain (technical detail out of scope here) |
| Giờ sinh (Địa Chi) | Used to fix the final position of Cung Mệnh; the single highest-precision-demanding of the four inputs |
| Giới tính | Affects the forward/reverse direction of certain auxiliary star cycles (e.g., Đại Vận) |

**Script-ready material (added 2026-07-24, conceptual only — see Follow-up Gap-Closure Research Note above):** a safe visual metaphor for the general *shape* of an sao, confirmed via cross-source research as structurally accurate without requiring any exact formula: picture **two "anchor" stars — Tử Vi and Thiên Phủ — each radiating its own chain of stars outward around the wheel in opposite rotations** (one chain running counter-clockwise from Tử Vi, the other running clockwise from Thiên Phủ), forming a kind of mirrored symmetry across the two halves of the chart. This is useful narration for conveying "there is a real, ordered structure here, not arbitrary placement" **without** walking through the counting/remainder arithmetic itself. This is additive color only — it does **not** loosen or replace any caution below; it must not be paired with any actual worked calculation from a real or invited birth date.

**Production cautions (highest-risk section of this packet's structural material):**
- **This is the single highest-risk structural topic in the packet.** Never script this as "enter your birth year/month/day/hour here and let's read it together" — this is precisely the "unlicensed personal-prediction service" pattern `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4 prohibits outright.
- Never present the counting procedure as one universally fixed formula identical across all schools; use conceptual language ("về nguyên tắc chung...") rather than "công thức chính xác là..."
- Do not go deeper into chính tinh/phụ tinh placement technique in any content sourced from this packet alone — that requires a dedicated, separately-researched packet.
- **Reaffirmed 2026-07-24, after dedicated follow-up research into the an sao algorithm's general shape:** this caution is explicitly kept, not loosened. The follow-up research confirmed the algorithm's general structure is real and consistent across sources, but also confirmed the edge-case/remainder-handling details genuinely vary by school — precise enough detail to let a viewer self-calculate their own chart remains out of scope for this packet regardless of how well-confirmed the general shape is. The new "two anchor stars" metaphor above is the only piece of additive script material this research pass authorizes; it does not authorize walking through the counting arithmetic itself.
- **Any illustrative example of chart construction must use a clearly fictional or long-deceased historical/legendary figure, explicitly labeled as an illustrative example**, with invented or clearly-marked-hypothetical birth data ("giả sử một người sinh giờ Tý, tháng Ba âm lịch — đây là ví dụ kỹ thuật, không phải lá số thật"). See "Illustrative Examples Only" under Risk-Specific Editorial Rules below.

## 12 Cung (12 Houses)

**Knowledge function:** 12 Cung are the fixed "slots" on the chart wheel, each mapped to one of the 12 Địa Chi positions, each traditionally associated with one life domain. Cung Mệnh is located first (see above); the remaining 11 follow it around the wheel in a fixed sequence.

**Note on ordering:** because the 12 Cung sit on a closed ring, sources may list them clockwise or counter-clockwise from Cung Mệnh — this produces two "reversed-looking" lists describing the identical structure, not a school disagreement. The listing below follows the sequence corroborated by 2 of 3 sources cross-checked (hocvienlyso.org, lichngaytot.com).

**Primary concepts / table (names high confidence across 3 sources; dual naming noted where documented):**

| # | Tên Cung | Tên gọi khác | Ý nghĩa truyền thống (khái quát) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mệnh | — | Central house; traditionally read as an overview of personality and overall life-pattern tendency |
| 2 | Huynh Đệ | — | Sibling relationships; some readings extend it to peer relationships generally |
| 3 | Phu Thê | — | Marriage, spousal relationship, traditionally-read partnership traits/affinity |
| 4 | Tử Tức | Tử Nữ | Children — traditionally read for number, affinity, and parent-child relationship themes |
| 5 | Tài Bạch | — | Money, wealth, how wealth is traditionally said to be earned/managed (§6 DOMAIN_GUIDE hard rule applies) |
| 6 | Tật Ách | — | Health/illness themes (**highest-sensitivity Cung in this domain — §7-8 DOMAIN_GUIDE apply at maximum caution**) |
| 7 | Thiên Di | — | Movement, relocation, change of living/working environment, travel abroad |
| 8 | Nô Bộc | Giao Hữu | Friends, subordinates/colleagues, helpers (older term reflects a pre-modern class register; "Giao Hữu" is the modernized alternative) |
| 9 | Quan Lộc | Sự Nghiệp | Career path, professional standing, public role |
| 10 | Điền Trạch | — | Housing, land, real estate, dwelling |
| 11 | Phúc Đức | — | Traditionally-read fortune/blessing, spiritual/inner life, ancestral spiritual legacy |
| 12 | Phụ Mẫu | — | Relationship with and circumstances of one's parents |

**Additional note:** some schools/sources also discuss "Cung Thân," which co-locates with one of the above 12 Cung depending on birth hour rather than existing as a 13th independent house. It is a genuine concept but requires its own dedicated research pass; it is deliberately not folded into the 12-Cung table above, to avoid implying "13 Cung."

**Script-ready material:** framing the 12 Cung as "a wheel of 12 life domains," comparable to other familiar 12-part cultural systems (12 con giáp, 12 months), is safe educational framing. Illustrating a specific Cung's traditional meaning through a clearly fictional or long-deceased legendary/historical figure ("Cung Quan Lộc của một vị quan trong truyện xưa được xem là...") is a safe way to teach the concept without individualized reading.

**Production cautions:**
- **Cung Tật Ách:** never describe as predicting a specific illness or a specific timing — only "theo quan niệm truyền thống, Cung này được xem là gắn với chủ đề sức khỏe" (§7-8, the domain's highest-sensitivity category).
- **Cung Mệnh, Phu Thê, Tử Tức:** highest risk of being pulled into "reading" the viewer — every illustrative example must anchor to a clearly fictional/historical character (see Risk-Specific rules below).
- **Cung Tài Bạch:** never translate into specific investment/financial advice (§6 DOMAIN_GUIDE, hard rule).
- Do not present one clockwise/counter-clockwise listing as "the correct one" and another as wrong — both describe the same ring structure (see note above).

# 14 Chính Tinh

Cross-checked against 3 Vietnamese sources (amlich.vn, tracuutuvi.com) and Chinese/English sources (Wikipedia EN, multiple Chinese-language pages): **the 14 star names and their Bắc Đẩu/Nam Đẩu group membership are consistent across every source checked.** **The Ngũ Hành (element) assigned to each star shows meaningful disagreement between schools/sources for at least 5 of the 14 stars** (Thiên Cơ, Thiên Đồng, Thiên Tướng, Thiên Lương, Thất Sát most notably, plus Tham Lang's dual-element attribution) — this is a genuine cross-source variance, not a simple error to resolve, and must never be presented as one settled table.

The table below uses the element assignment most common across the Vietnamese sources cross-checked (amlich.vn and tracuutuvi.com largely agree), with variant notes recorded explicitly per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3's instruction to name the school/source when schools disagree.

**Structural grouping:** the 14 chính tinh split into two "tinh hệ" (star systems) — the Tử Vi system (6 stars, generally called "Bắc Đẩu" group) and the Thiên Phủ system (8 stars, generally called "Nam Đẩu" group). This Bắc Đẩu/Nam Đẩu labeling is a modern Vietnamese-language convention, not a modern astronomical classification.

# Risk-Specific Editorial Rules

This section restates and localizes `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md`'s rules specifically for Tử Vi content built from this packet. It does not replace the Domain Guide — it is the packet-level enforcement layer, the same relationship `KP_BUD_001` maintains with `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md`.

## Core Boundary Rule (§4) — Explaining the Tradition Is Not Performing a Reading

The channel may explain, in general educational terms, what a star, house, or chart pattern traditionally means, and may illustrate how a lá số is traditionally constructed and read. **The channel must never perform an individualized reading for a specific named or implied viewer.** No content sourced from this packet may calculate an actual chart on-screen and tell a viewer (named, implied, "you," or a family member) what it means for their actual life, and no content may invite viewers to submit birth details for a personal answer.

## Illustrative Examples Only

**Every illustrative example of a lá số, a Cung Mệnh calculation, or a specific Cung's meaning anywhere in this Knowledge Packet, and in every future script or visual derived from it, must use a clearly fictional character or a long-deceased historical/legendary figure explicitly identified as an illustrative example.** This applies without exception to:

- Any worked example of the chart-construction process (four birth inputs → 12-Cung wheel).
- Any worked example of what a specific Cung "means" for an illustrative chart.
- Any on-screen visual of a sample lá số — which must carry an explicit on-screen label such as "LÁ SỐ MINH HỌA — NHÂN VẬT HƯ CẤU" (illustrative chart — fictional character) so that no viewer, even one skimming quickly, mistakes it for a real chart or their own.

**Never** use birth data that could be mistaken for, or invite comparison with, a real viewer's actual birth date. **Never** frame a walkthrough as "your chart." **Never** use second person ("bạn") attached to a specific chart outcome — the only acceptable register is "theo Tử Vi truyền thống, Cung X thường được xem là..." per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §5.

## Lifespan & Serious-Event Prediction Rule (§8 — highest-sensitivity rule in this domain)

This restates, without softening, `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §8, because Tử Vi's traditional frameworks do include lifespan-adjacent and misfortune-adjacent houses/stars (most directly Cung Tật Ách, but potentially any house/star combination a practitioner tradition treats as ill-omened):

- **Never** state or imply a specific death timing, health-crisis timing, or major-misfortune timing for any real or implied person — including the viewer, a family member, or a public figure.
- **Never**, even using a clearly fictional or historical/legendary illustrative character, construct an example of the form "nhân vật X có Cung Tật Ách xấu nên mất năm Y tuổi Z." This form is prohibited **even for admittedly fictional characters**, because the narrative pattern itself teaches the viewer how to self-apply a mortality reading — the harm is in the *shape* of the content, not just in whether the subject is real.
- **Do not** produce content whose hook or structure invites a viewer to calculate their own lifespan-adjacent chart elements and self-apply a mortality reading.
- **Do** discuss lifespan/misfortune-adjacent houses and stars historically and structurally — what the tradition says they represent, why the tradition includes them, how different schools have historically softened or reinterpreted this territory over time — without ever landing on an applied personal or even applied-fictional claim.
- If in doubt, escalate caution rather than escalate specificity, exactly as `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §8 instructs (mirroring `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §34's crisis-handling posture).

## Prediction & Certainty (§5)

- **Do** use traditional register: "theo Tử Vi truyền thống, cung X kết hợp sao Y thường được xem là..."
- **Do not** use "bạn sẽ..." about any specific future outcome, to any specific or implied viewer.
- **Do** acknowledge openly that different schools/practitioners read the same chart differently (this packet's own 14-Chính-Tinh Ngũ Hành table is a ready-made example of this plurality).
- **Do not** claim any reading is scientifically proven or statistically validated.

## Financial Boundary (§6)

Cung Tài Bạch content may explain the traditional cultural meaning of the "wealth house" as belief/heritage. It must **never** translate into specific investment, trading, or business advice tied to a chart reading, and must **never** frame a Tử Vi-derived action as a guaranteed mechanism for financial gain.

## Health Boundary (§7)

Cung Tật Ách and any health-adjacent star/house content may be explained as traditional cultural belief connecting a house to health/vitality themes. It must **never** diagnose, predict a specific health event, or claim any Tử Vi practice can cure, prevent, or replace medical treatment.

## Anti-Fear-Sales Standard (§9)

No content built from this packet may use manufactured urgency, dread, or threat framing (e.g., implying a bad Cung "will" bring disaster unless the viewer buys/does something) to drive engagement, subscriptions, or sales. Traditional beliefs about fortune/misfortune should be handled with the same calm, compassionate, non-exploitative voice `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §13 establishes for the Buddhism domain.
