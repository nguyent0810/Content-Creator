# Series Bible: Giải Mã Tử Vi Phong Thủy

## Document Role

This Series Bible is the permanent strategic constitution for the long-form YouTube series **Giải Mã Tử Vi Phong Thủy**. It governs every future episode, season, research packet, creative packet, script, visual plan, voiceover direction, review pass, and expansion product connected to this series.

This document is not a script, not a content calendar, not a prompt, and not a loose episode planner. It defines the identity, philosophy, educational journey, emotional arc, season design, episode architecture, continuity rules, and long-term evolution of one complete Tử Vi & Phong Thủy education series.

The series belongs to the FENG_SHUI domain (`FS`) and must obey `DOMAINS/FENG_SHUI/DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` in full, without exception or softening. It must protect cultural and historical accuracy, source discipline, epistemic honesty, non-exploitation, and long-term consistency. It must never use Tử Vi or Phong Thủy as a fear product, a guaranteed-fortune product, a personal-reading service, a financial-advice product, or an algorithmic spectacle.

This series is written in the same spirit as `DOMAINS/BUDDHISM/SERIES_BIBLES/SB_BUD_001_Kinh_Dia_Tang.md` and mirrors its structure, but it governs a materially different kind of content territory. Kinh Địa Tạng has one canonical scripture; Tử Vi and Phong Thủy are living traditions with multiple competing schools and no single canonical text (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2). More importantly, this series carries a structural risk that the Buddhism domain does not carry in the same form: the risk of the channel accidentally functioning as an unlicensed personal-prediction service (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4). Every section of this document must be read with that risk foregrounded.

The series must help viewers approach Tử Vi and Phong Thủy with curiosity, historical humility, cultural respect, and critical discernment. It must teach both traditions as structured, centuries-old East Asian and Vietnamese systems of symbolic thought — worth understanding on their own terms — while never presenting them as scientifically proven, never performing an individualized reading for any specific or implied viewer, and never using fear or manufactured urgency to sell anything.

# Series Identity

## Series Name

**Giải Mã Tử Vi Phong Thủy**

The canonical English working description is: **Decoding Tử Vi and Phong Thủy — Two Traditions, Explained with Context, Discernment, and Respect**.

The Vietnamese name should be used in public series branding. English phrasing may be used internally for research, metadata planning, and cross-language expansion. The series name must not be turned into sensational variations such as "bí mật phong thủy ít ai biết", "xem vận mệnh của bạn ngay", "cách hóa giải vận xui tức thì", or "giải mã lá số của bạn trong 5 phút". Any title pattern that implies the channel will calculate or reveal a specific viewer's personal fate is a direct violation of `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4 and must be rejected at the title stage, not caught later in review.

## Mission

The mission of **Giải Mã Tử Vi Phong Thủy** is to guide viewers through the structural logic, history, and cultural meaning of Tử Vi Đẩu Số and Phong Thủy, while correcting both of the two opposite failure modes that dominate existing Vietnamese-language content in this space: content that treats these traditions as unquestionable, guaranteed, fear-driven certainty, and content that dismisses them entirely as ignorant superstition.

The series must help viewers understand:

- why Âm Dương and Ngũ Hành function as a shared symbolic grammar underneath both Tử Vi and Phong Thủy, not as two unrelated systems;
- how a lá số (birth chart) or a floor plan is traditionally constructed and read, without ever constructing or reading one for the viewer personally;
- why multiple schools (Loan Đầu vs. Lý Khí; Bát Trạch vs. Huyền Không Phi Tinh) can look at the same house and disagree, and why that disagreement is honest rather than a flaw to be hidden;
- how historical attributions (Phục Hy, Văn Vương, Trần Đoàn, Quách Phác, Hoàng Bính) function as tradition and legend rather than as verified biography;
- how commercial Phong Thủy media in Vietnam has a documented pattern of using fear and manufactured urgency to sell objects and consultations, and why this channel refuses that pattern completely;
- how modern viewers can engage with these traditions as cultural heritage, as reflective frameworks, or as objects of historical curiosity, without the channel needing to adjudicate whether they are empirically "true."

The mission is educational, cultural, historical, and reflective. It is not a divination service, not a personal-reading service, not a financial or medical advisory practice, and not a product marketplace.

## Core Promise

The core promise of the series is:

**This series will help viewers understand Tử Vi and Phong Thủy with accuracy, cultural respect, and critical clarity, so they can move from either blind belief or reflexive dismissal into informed, discerning engagement with these traditions.**

Every episode must deliver some part of this promise. If an episode only explains structure but does not build discernment, it is incomplete. If an episode entertains but blurs the boundary between "explaining a tradition" and "performing a reading," it is unsafe. If an episode increases attention by frightening the viewer about their own fortune, health, or lifespan, it violates the series at the deepest level.

## Core Transformation

The intended transformation is a gradual movement:

1. From treating Phong Thủy/Tử Vi as either absolute truth or pure nonsense, toward understanding them as structured cultural-symbolic systems.
2. From fear of bad luck or misfortune toward calm, historically grounded understanding.
3. From seeking a personal verdict toward understanding how a tradition works in general.
4. From consuming fear-sales content unknowingly toward recognizing its patterns and refusing them.
5. From assuming one school is "the correct one" toward understanding plurality of interpretation as honest, not evasive.
6. From vague terminology toward precise, glossary-consistent vocabulary.
7. From uncertainty about what is historical fact versus legend toward comfort holding tiered confidence.
8. From treating vật phẩm phong thủy as guaranteed mechanisms toward understanding them as cultural symbols of aspiration.
9. From passive superstition or passive skepticism toward active, selective, informed application in daily life.
10. From isolated fragments of folk knowledge toward a coherent map of both traditions' shared roots and distinct branches.

The series should not promise fortune, wealth, health, protection from misfortune, romantic success, or any guaranteed outcome — traditional, symbolic, or otherwise. Its transformation is educational and cultural: a clearer map, a more discerning eye, a healthier relationship with inherited belief, and protection from exploitation.

## Target Audience

The primary audience includes Vietnamese-speaking viewers who have grown up around Phong Thủy practices (hướng nhà, bàn thờ, vật phẩm) and Tử Vi references (lá số, "xem tuổi") in family and cultural life, but who do not have a structured understanding of where these systems come from, how they actually work, or where their boundaries lie.

The secondary audience includes viewers who are actively curious about Tử Vi or Phong Thủy as intellectual or cultural systems — including skeptics who want a fair, non-mystical explanation of the internal logic, and believers who want a deeper, more accurate account than commercial content typically provides.

The tertiary audience includes viewers drawn to East Asian cultural history, symbolic systems, and comparative traditions more broadly, who may not personally practice or believe in either system but find the historical and structural content valuable in its own right.

The series must be accessible to complete beginners while maintaining enough structural depth for viewers who already know fragments of either system. Advanced historical and comparative episodes may exist, but they must be clearly scaffolded and must never assume the viewer wants a personal reading.

## Viewer Maturity Levels

### Beginning Viewer

The beginning viewer may know isolated fragments: a tỳ hưu on a shelf, a warning about "hướng nhà xấu," a relative's lá số being "xem" before a wedding, a vague sense that Phong Thủy is either powerful or fake. They need orientation, safety from both fear and mockery, and basic shared vocabulary (Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Bát Quái).

The series must help them understand what these traditions are, where they come from, why the channel will never calculate a personal reading for them, and how to tell careful content from exploitative content.

### Intermediate Viewer

The intermediate viewer may already know the basic vocabulary, may have had a lá số read by a practitioner, or may apply some Phong Thủy principles at home. They need structure: the difference between schools, the difference between structural consensus and school-specific rules, and correction of common conflations (e.g., mixing Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh into one system).

The series must help them connect Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Bát Quái, 12 Cung, and the specific school logic of Phong Thủy into one coherent map, always labeled by source and school.

### Advanced Viewer

The advanced viewer may be interested in the historical debates, the competing lineages, the cross-source disagreements (such as the Ngũ Hành attribution disputes among the 14 Chính Tinh), and the plurality of Vietnamese and Chinese textual traditions. They need source discipline, tiered historical confidence, and comparison without declaring a winner.

The series must give them deeper frameworks without pretending to resolve debates that the source material itself does not resolve (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2-3).

## Educational Objectives

The series must teach viewers:

- the shared foundational grammar of Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, and Bát Quái that underlies both Tử Vi and Phong Thủy;
- the structural building blocks of Tử Vi: Thiên Can, Địa Chi, the 12 Cung, and the 14 Chính Tinh, at the conceptual and historical level, never as a personal-reading tool;
- the structural building blocks of Phong Thủy: Loan Đầu (form school) and Lý Khí (compass school, including Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh), always labeled by school;
- the difference between cross-school consensus (Tier 1), school-specific rules (Tier 2), popular contemporary practice (Tier 3), and historical/academic scholarship (Tier 4), per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3;
- the historical layering of founding legends (Phục Hy, Văn Vương, Trần Đoàn, Quách Phác, Hoàng Bính) as tradition and lore, tiered honestly by confidence;
- how to recognize fear-sales patterns in commercial Phong Thủy/Tử Vi media and why this channel rejects them;
- the difference between explaining a tradition and performing a reading — the single most important distinction in the entire series;
- how to hold cultural respect and epistemic honesty at the same time, without either mocking the tradition or presenting it as unquestionable fact.

## Reflective and Cultural Objectives

Unlike the Buddhism domain, this series does not cultivate devotional or spiritual practice as its central aim — Tử Vi and Phong Thủy are not treated here as religious paths but as cultural-symbolic systems (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §1). The series should instead cultivate:

- intellectual curiosity about how a centuries-old symbolic system holds together internally, on its own terms;
- respect for the people — ancestors, family members, practitioners — who have engaged with these traditions sincerely across generations, without needing to validate or invalidate their belief;
- calm, non-anxious engagement with questions of luck, fortune, and misfortune;
- discernment as a life skill: the ability to separate cultural heritage from commercial exploitation;
- humility before genuine historical uncertainty, rather than a need to resolve every open question;
- a healthier, less transactional relationship with objects, directions, and rituals associated with these traditions;
- comfort holding multiple valid layers of meaning — cultural/historical, traditional-belief, and modern-reflective — without collapsing them into one (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §10).

# Series Philosophy

## Why This Series Exists

Tử Vi and Phong Thủy are among the most commercially exploited cultural traditions in Vietnamese media. A viewer searching for basic information is far more likely to encounter a vật phẩm storefront disguised as an educational video, a fear-driven "hóa giải ngay kẻo muộn" sales pitch, or a self-declared "thầy" issuing confident individual verdicts, than a calm, source-disciplined explanation of how either system actually works.

This series exists because these traditions deserve better than either extreme currently on offer. They deserve patient structural explanation, honest historical tiering, and a firm, permanent refusal to perform the one thing that makes so much existing content exploitative: telling a specific viewer what their specific chart or their specific house means for their specific life.

The series also exists because modern Vietnamese viewers are caught between two unhelpful postures: uncritical belief that treats every claim as certain, and reflexive dismissal that treats the entire tradition as ignorant. Neither posture serves the viewer. This series must open a third path: understand the systems as they are — internally coherent, historically layered, plural across schools, culturally meaningful, and epistemically bounded.

## What Problems It Solves

The series solves the problem of fragmented vocabulary. Viewers often know isolated terms — Âm Dương, mệnh, hướng nhà, cung, sao — without knowing how they connect into a system.

The series solves the problem of false certainty. Commercial content routinely presents one school's rule as the universal answer. This series must always name the school and acknowledge plurality.

The series solves the problem of fear-based sales. The channel must actively counter the "nếu không làm điều này, vận xui sẽ ập đến" pattern that dominates the vật phẩm and hóa giải market (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §9), and must show viewers how to recognize this pattern elsewhere.

The series solves the problem of the individualized-reading trap. Many well-intentioned educational channels slide from "here is how a lá số works" into "let me read your chart" because it is more engaging. This series must never make that slide, in any episode, at any season.

The series solves the problem of collapsed historical claims. Legendary founder stories are routinely presented as settled fact. This series must consistently tier confidence and use "tương truyền" framing.

The series solves the problem of financial and health overreach. Cung Tài Bạch and Cung Tật Ách content, and Phong Thủy wealth-corner or health-direction content, must never become investment advice or medical claims.

## What Misunderstandings It Corrects

The series must repeatedly correct these misunderstandings:

- Phong Thủy and Tử Vi are scientifically proven systems.
- Phong Thủy and Tử Vi are pure nonsense with no internal logic or cultural value.
- A lá số is a fixed, unchangeable record of a person's entire destiny.
- Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh are two steps of one system rather than two independent lineages that can disagree.
- Tiên Thiên Bát Quái and Hậu Thiên Bát Quái are interchangeable.
- Buying the right vật phẩm guarantees wealth, health, or protection.
- A Phong Thủy layout or a Tử Vi chart can predict the specific timing of death, illness, or catastrophe for a real or an illustrative person.
- Trần Đoàn's authorship of Tử Vi, or Phục Hy's and Văn Vương's authorship of Bát Quái, are confirmed historical events rather than traditional attributions.
- Every commercial Phong Thủy consultant or object seller is a legitimate transmitter of the tradition rather than, in a well-documented subset of cases, a participant in a spiritual-scam pattern.
- Understanding a tradition's internal logic requires believing it is literally, empirically true.

Each correction must be made with respect for cultural heritage and for the people who hold these beliefs sincerely. The series corrects confusion and exploitation, not people. It should never mock family devotion, elders' beliefs, or genuine practitioners operating in good faith.

## What Emotional Needs It Serves

The series serves viewers who need:

- calm, structured understanding instead of anxiety about luck and fortune;
- relief from fear-based commercial spiritual media;
- language to explain family traditions (bàn thờ, hướng bếp, "xem tuổi" before marriage) that they inherited but never had explained;
- protection from spiritual-scam patterns targeting vulnerable or anxious people;
- permission to be curious about a tradition without being pressured to either fully believe or fully reject it;
- a way to honor cultural heritage without being manipulated by it;
- confidence to recognize and walk away from manufactured urgency ("mua ngay kẻo muộn," "3 ngày nữa hết hạn hóa giải").

The series must be intellectually engaging but never emotionally predatory. It may evoke wonder, nostalgia, or curiosity, but never dread about the viewer's own fate.

## What Reflective and Cultural Needs It Serves

The series serves the need to understand inherited cultural practice without either uncritical acceptance or contemptuous dismissal. Many viewers stand between a grandparent who consulted a Tử Vi practitioner before every major life decision and a modern, skeptical self that does not know what to do with that inheritance. This tension is the series' emotional center.

The series also serves the need to engage thoughtfully with questions of luck, fortune, and misfortune that every culture grapples with in some form — not by resolving whether these systems are "real," but by giving viewers a clear, honest account of what the tradition claims, how it developed, and where its internal disagreements lie.

The series serves the need to consume media critically. Karma-adjacent fear (in the Buddhism domain) and misfortune-adjacent fear (in this domain) are both exploited by low-quality media; this series models a calmer, more honest alternative for both.

# Series Tone

## Voice

The voice of the series is curious, precise, culturally warm, and source-aware. It should feel like a well-researched documentary guide who respects the tradition, respects the viewer's intelligence, and never claims personal authority to read anyone's fate.

The voice must not sound like:

- a fortune-teller issuing verdicts;
- a salesperson pushing vật phẩm;
- a fear-based "thầy" manufacturing urgency;
- a condescending skeptic mocking belief;
- a motivational speaker;
- a horror or mystery-thriller narrator;
- an academic lecturer detached from cultural meaning;
- a self-declared authority claiming exclusive or secret knowledge.

## Emotion

The emotional palette is curious, warm, grounded, occasionally wry (when correcting an obvious commercial exaggeration), and respectful. The series may include mild suspense when unpacking a historical mystery or a cross-school disagreement, but this suspense must resolve into clarity, not into dread about the viewer's own life.

Emotion must move through arcs. A strong episode should not remain purely academic from beginning to end, nor purely entertaining without substance. It may begin with a familiar cultural image (a tỳ hưu, a lá số, a house facing "wrong"), deepen into structural explanation, turn toward historical or cross-school nuance, and close with a grounded, non-mystical takeaway.

## Narration

Narration should be clear enough for audio-first comprehension. Sentences should carry one idea at a time, especially when explaining Ngũ Hành cycles, Bát Quái tables, Kua number formulas, or star names — this domain has an unusually high density of technical vocabulary compared to general reflective content.

Canonical narration habits:

- define before applying;
- name the school before stating a rule;
- source before asserting;
- use traditional register ("theo Tử Vi truyền thống, cung X thường được xem là...") instead of certainty register ("bạn sẽ...");
- flag legend as legend ("tương truyền...");
- close technical segments with a plain-language recap.

## Pacing

The canonical pacing is structured documentary-explainer pacing — brisker and more information-dense than the Buddhism series' contemplative pacing, because this domain's core value proposition is structural clarity rather than devotional atmosphere. The viewer should feel guided through a system, not rushed past technical detail and not buried in it.

Pacing must vary by topic:

- Foundational concept episodes (Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Bát Quái) require careful, segmented explanation with visual reinforcement.
- School-comparison episodes (Bát Trạch vs. Huyền Không) require deliberately slower pacing so the "two independent systems" point lands clearly.
- Historical/legend episodes require narrative pacing with clear tier-labeling at each turn.
- Applied-life episodes (hướng nhà, bàn thờ, vật phẩm) require practical, grounded pacing with explicit anti-fear-sales framing.
- High-sensitivity topics (Cung Tật Ách, lifespan-adjacent content) require the slowest, most protective pacing in the series, with caution escalating rather than specificity escalating, exactly as `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §8 requires.

## Music

Music should be warm, culturally grounded, and unobtrusive. It may use traditional Vietnamese and East Asian instrumentation (đàn tranh, guzheng-adjacent textures, soft percussion) blended with restrained modern documentary scoring. It must not become mystical-thriller music, horror-adjacent suspense scoring, or hard-sell infomercial music.

Music should support:

- calm curiosity;
- documentary clarity;
- cultural warmth;
- gentle emphasis during historical narrative;
- restraint during dense structural explanation, so words have room.

Music must never manufacture dread — no low drones under lifespan-adjacent content, no ominous stingers under "hóa giải" segments, no suspense scoring that implies the viewer should be worried about their own fortune.

## Visual Identity

The visual identity of **Giải Mã Tử Vi Phong Thủy** is precise, warm, and symbol-literate. It should feel like a well-produced cultural-history documentary, not a horoscope app, not a horror channel, and not a product infomercial.

Primary visual world:

- Bát Quái diagrams and Ngũ Hành wheels, rendered as clean, elegant motion graphics rather than mystical glowing icons;
- lá số grids and địa bàn wheels, always visually labeled as illustrative/fictional when populated with example data;
- traditional Vietnamese architecture (nhà tựa núi nhìn sông, mái ngói, sân vườn) for Loan Đầu content;
- old books, hand-drawn charts, calligraphy, aged paper for historical/legend segments;
- compass and la bàn imagery for Lý Khí content;
- domestic scenes (bàn thờ, bếp, cửa chính) shown respectfully and practically, without theatrical mysticism;
- market and storefront imagery, shown critically, for anti-fear-sales and vật phẩm literacy segments;
- muted, tasteful use of tỳ hưu, thiềm thừ, and similar objects as cultural artifacts, never as glowing "magic" props.

## Color Language

The canonical color language draws on the Ngũ Hành palette itself, used as an organizing visual device rather than mystical decoration: jade/forest green (Mộc), warm red-orange (Hỏa), earth ochre and warm yellow (Thổ), muted white and pewter (Kim), deep indigo and charcoal-blue (Thủy). Gold accents may be used sparingly for emphasis, not luxury signaling. Deep red should signify cultural warmth or traditional ritual, never alarm.

Avoid one-note palettes. Do not let the series become dominated by mystical purple-and-gold "fortune-telling app" aesthetics, or by horror-adjacent black-and-red. Each episode should draw its dominant color primarily from the Ngũ Hành element most relevant to its topic, keeping the overall series visually varied and legible.

## Cinematic Style

The cinematic style should combine:

- documentary clarity;
- clean structural motion graphics (wheels, grids, tables) that make technical content watchable;
- symbolic recurrence (the Ngũ Hành wheel, the Bát Quái octagon, the 12-Cung wheel as recurring visual anchors);
- restrained historical dramatization for legend segments, clearly framed as illustrative;
- explicit on-screen labeling for any illustrative lá số or floor-plan example ("VÍ DỤ MINH HỌA — NHÂN VẬT HƯ CẤU", per `KP_FS_002`'s Illustrative Examples Only rule);
- source cards and school-attribution cards whenever a specific rule is stated;
- chapter markers for structurally dense episodes.

The camera should observe cultural objects and practices with respect and without theatrical mystification. Illustrative charts and floor plans should be shown with the same visual discipline as a textbook diagram, not as a mysterious revelation.

## Forbidden Styles

Forbidden styles include:

- performing or simulating an individualized reading for a specific or implied *living private* viewer, on-screen or via any invitation to submit birth data or floor plans (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4). This does **not** forbid two explicitly permitted formats defined in §4a: zodiac-year cohort content ("12 con giáp," weekly/monthly/seasonal — a strong Shorts format) and retrospective case studies of documented, deceased, or public historical figures (a credibility-building format for Long-form). Both remain subject to every other rule below;
- second-person certainty language about a specific future outcome ("bạn sẽ...") (§5);
- presenting Phong Thủy or Tử Vi as scientifically proven or empirically validated (§5, §12);
- financial, investment, trading, or business advice framed through a chart or layout reading (§6);
- framing any object, direction, or ritual as a guaranteed mechanism for financial gain (§6);
- diagnosing illness, predicting a specific health event, or claiming any practice cures, prevents, or replaces medical treatment (§7);
- stating or implying specific death, health-crisis, or major-misfortune timing for any real, implied, or even clearly fictional illustrative person (§8 — this restriction applies to fictional examples too, because the narrative shape itself teaches self-application);
- manufactured urgency, dread, or threat framing to drive engagement, subscriptions, or sales — "nếu không làm điều này, vận xui sẽ ập đến," "mua ngay kẻo muộn," "chỉ 3 ngày nữa hết hạn hóa giải" and equivalents (§9);
- presenting one school's rule as the single universally correct answer, or silently blending Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh into one invented "combined formula" (§2-3, §10);
- mocking or dismissing the tradition as ignorant superstition, or presenting it as unquestionable fact — either extreme violates §10 and §12;
- fake ancient-secret aesthetics, glowing mystical iconography, horoscope-app gamification, or "hotline gọi ngay để giải hạn" infomercial framing;
- inventing a source, a school name, a star name, or a numeric rule not found in an approved Knowledge Packet (§3, §12);
- horror editing, jump-scare sound design, or supernatural-thriller visual grammar applied to misfortune or hell-adjacent Phong Thủy content;
- treating grief, illness, family anxiety, or financial hardship as an engagement hook.

## Editorial Posture: Transform, Don't Just Refuse

Every writer, researcher, and QA agent working in this series operates as **an editor who transforms risk, not a gatekeeper who refuses** (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §14, added 2026-07-17 at the channel owner's direction). §6, §7, and §8 lock specific *claim types* (personalized investment advice, medical diagnosis/cure claims, living-person mortality timing) — they do not lock the *topics* of money, health, or life-cycle/mortality, which all remain rich, open creative territory. When an idea, hook, or draft line touches one of these locks: name exactly what's invalid, keep everything else (hook, emotion, mystery, structure), transform the invalid part into the nearest valid framing, and deliver a complete finished output — never stop the pipeline to relitigate the boundary, and never let a transform flatten a script into vague, hedged, generically "safe" writing. A script that comes back thin or watered-down has failed this series' editorial standard as surely as one that crosses a hard boundary.

# Canonical Boundaries

## Required Knowledge Packets

The two foundational sources for this series are **KP_FS_001_Phong_Thuy.md** and **KP_FS_002_Tu_Vi.md**, as represented in `DOMAINS/FENG_SHUI/KNOWLEDGE_PACKETS/`. Both packets are currently status `draft-pending-human-review` and have not yet passed Domain QA, Research QA, Safety QA, or Historical QA (per each packet's own QA Status table). Production scripts drawn from this Series Bible must track each packet's live QA status and must not be treated as fully cleared for production merely because this Series Bible references them.

Episodes may reference Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Bát Quái, Loan Đầu, Bát Trạch, Huyền Không Phi Tinh (from KP_FS_001), and Thiên Can, Địa Chi, the 12 Cung, and the 14 Chính Tinh (from KP_FS_002) only within the confidence tiers, school labels, and production cautions each packet already specifies. Future research packets may add detail (e.g., a dedicated star-placement packet, a bàn thờ gia tiên ethnographic packet) but must not contradict `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md`.

## School and Lineage Boundaries

Neither Phong Thủy nor Tử Vi has one canonical text or one authoritative school (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2). Every specific rule stated in a script must name its school or lineage:

- **Phong Thủy:** Loan Đầu (Hình Thế phái) vs. Lý Khí (Compass phái), and within Lý Khí, Bát Trạch (Tam Hợp lineage) vs. Huyền Không Phi Tinh (Tam Nguyên lineage). These are independent systems that can disagree on the same house; no episode may present one as more correct than the other or invent a reconciliation between them.
- **Tử Vi:** the Bắc Đẩu/Nam Đẩu star-group structure is cross-school consensus, but Ngũ Hành attribution disagrees across sources for at least 5 of the 14 Chính Tinh (Thiên Cơ, Thiên Đồng, Thiên Tướng, Thiên Lương, Thất Sát, plus Tham Lang's dual attribution) — any script using the full star table must disclose this variance rather than presenting a single silent column as truth.
- Vietnamese-specific lineages (Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang's Quốc Ngữ tradition, cụ Thiên Lương's Nghiệm Lý school) must be named as Vietnamese-specific development, distinct from mainland Chinese source tradition, per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2.

## Source Priority Hierarchy

Reproduced from `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §3 and applied throughout this series:

1. Widely-attested classical structural rules (12 Cung structure, Ngũ Hành Tương Sinh/Tương Khắc cycles, Bát Quái's eight trigrams) that essentially all schools agree on — safe to state as settled.
2. School-specific or lineage-specific rules, explicitly labeled by school — never stated as universal.
3. Popular contemporary Vietnamese practice/commentary, cross-checked across at least 2-3 independent sources — usable for "how this is popularly understood and applied today," always framed as contemporary practice, not ancient law.
4. Academic/historical scholarship on origins and social history — used for the historical-framing layer only, never to assert empirical validation.

Do not invent a source, a school name, a star name, or a numeric rule. If a claim cannot be cross-checked across at least two independent sources, mark it explicitly as single-sourced/lower-confidence.

## Historical Confidence

Historical and founding-legend claims must be labeled by confidence level, mirroring the Buddhism domain's discipline:

- **High confidence:** cross-verified structural tables (Thiên Can, Địa Chi, 12 Cung names, 14 Chính Tinh names and grouping), the Ngũ Hành Tương Sinh/Tương Khắc cycles, the Hậu Thiên Bát Quái table used in home applications.
- **Medium confidence:** general historical formation windows (e.g., Tử Vi's Song-dynasty formation, Ngũ Hành's Warring States systematization), Thái Cực Đồ's graphical origin, individual Chính Tinh Ngũ Hành attributions where sources disagree.
- **Low confidence / legendary:** the Phục Hy and Văn Vương authorship attributions for the two Bát Quái tables, the Quách Phác/Táng Thư quotation, the Trần Đoàn founding-of-Tử-Vi attribution, the Lã Động Tân competing variant, the Hoàng Bính 1257 transmission-to-Vietnam story, and the Cục 2-3-4-5-6 numerological justification.

Do not dramatize low-confidence material as confirmed history. Legendary founding narratives may be honored as cultural lore without being presented as settled fact — always framed with "tương truyền" or equivalent.

## The Core Boundary (§4) — Explaining a Tradition Is Not Performing a Reading

This is the single most important structural rule governing this entire series, reproduced without softening from `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §4:

The series may explain, in general educational terms, what a star, house, direction, or element traditionally means, and may illustrate how a lá số or a floor plan is traditionally constructed and read. **The series must never perform an individualized reading for a specific named or implied viewer.** No episode may calculate an actual chart on-screen and tell a viewer what it means for their actual life, and no episode may invite viewers to submit birth details or floor plans for a personal answer. Any illustrative example must use a clearly fictional or historical/legendary figure, explicitly labeled, with data that cannot be mistaken for a real viewer's own.

## Prediction & Certainty Rules (§5)

Traditional epistemic register must be preserved. Scripts should say "theo Tử Vi truyền thống, cung X kết hợp sao Y thường được xem là..." and must never say "bạn sẽ..." about any specific future outcome. Scripts must acknowledge that different schools/practitioners can read the same chart or layout differently, and must never claim a reading is scientifically proven, statistically validated, or guaranteed accurate.

## Financial Boundary (§6, hard rule)

Cung Tài Bạch and Phong Thủy wealth-corner/prosperity-object content may explain traditional cultural meaning as belief and heritage. It must never give or imply specific investment, trading, business, or financial-decision advice tied to a chart or layout reading, and must never frame an object, direction, or ritual as a guaranteed mechanism for financial gain.

## Health & Medical Boundary (§7, hard rule)

Content connecting a house, direction, or element to health/vitality themes must be framed as cultural and historical belief, never as medical claim. The series must never diagnose an illness, predict a specific health event, or claim any practice can cure, prevent, or replace medical treatment.

## Death, Lifespan & Serious-Life-Event Boundary (§8, highest-sensitivity rule in this domain)

This is more sensitive than any single topic in the Buddhism domain's hell-realm content, because it risks a viewer applying a specific mortality claim to themselves or a loved one. The series must never state or imply a specific death timing, health-crisis timing, or major-misfortune timing for any real or implied person — and, per `KP_FS_002`, this prohibition extends to clearly fictional illustrative characters as well, because the narrative *shape* of such a claim teaches self-application regardless of whether the subject is real. Cung Tật Ách and any lifespan-adjacent house/star content may be discussed only historically and structurally. If in doubt, escalate caution rather than specificity.

## Anti-Fear-Sales Standard (§9)

Reproduced from `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §9 as a non-negotiable, channel-defining rule: no episode may use fear of bad luck, illness, or misfortune to sell objects, consultations, or subscriptions, and no episode may be structured around a manufactured deadline or threat. This series must actively model the alternative — calm, reverent, non-manipulative explanation — and, where relevant, name the fear-sales pattern explicitly so viewers can recognize it elsewhere.

## Symbolic, Historical & Cultural Interpretation Framework (§10)

Every substantive topic should be handled across three layers, none of which should be presented as erasing the others: the cultural/historical layer (where the practice came from, how it has been practiced across Vietnamese history), the traditional-belief layer (what practitioners within the tradition say an element means, on its own terms), and the modern-reflective layer (what a modern viewer might take from the practice's underlying logic, without asserting empirical validation). Do not say a practice is "actually just" psychology, and do not say it is "actually just" literal fact requiring no interpretation.

## Terminology Standards (§11)

Full glossary lives in `DOMAINS/FENG_SHUI/GLOSSARY/DOMAIN_GLOSSARY.md`. Core controlled terms include Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Bát Quái, Thiên Can, Địa Chi, Tử Vi Đẩu Số, Cung, Chính tinh / Phụ tinh, Mệnh, Thân, Tài Bạch, Phúc Đức, Loan Đầu, Lý Khí, Bát Trạch, Huyền Không Phi Tinh, hướng nhà, trạch mệnh. Scripts must not invent new terminology; a needed term absent from the glossary must be added there first.

## Terminology and Claims Never to Use (§12)

Never present Phong Thủy or Tử Vi as scientifically proven or empirically validated. Never claim the channel, host, or content can perform an authoritative personal reading. Never issue financial, medical, legal, or safety advice through a chart/layout frame. Never give specific mortality, illness, or major-misfortune timing. Never use manufactured urgency, fear, or threat to drive engagement or sales. Never mock or dismiss the tradition, and never present it as unquestionable fact.

## Topics Requiring Escalated Caution

High-caution topics requiring QA escalation and maximum protective framing include: Cung Tật Ách and any lifespan-adjacent house/star; any request-shaped content that could be mistaken for an invitation to submit personal birth data or a floor plan; vật phẩm phong thủy and the documented spiritual-scam pattern in the Vietnamese commercial market; bàn thờ gia tiên content (currently only Tier 3 sourced, no independent ethnographic source yet — see Long-term Knowledge Roadmap); Cung Tài Bạch and any wealth-corner content; historical attribution segments where legend and fact are easily conflated; any segment comparing Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh on the same illustrative house.

# Audience Journey

## Beginning Viewer

The beginning viewer should enter through orientation, shared vocabulary, and trust-building. Early episodes should answer: What is Phong Thủy? What is Tử Vi? Why do they share a root grammar? Why will this channel never read my personal chart or house? How do I tell careful content from exploitative content?

The beginning viewer needs:

- simple diagrams (Ngũ Hành wheel, Bát Quái octagon);
- plain-language definitions;
- explicit, early reassurance about the individualized-reading boundary;
- non-mystical visuals;
- practical, low-risk examples;
- permission to be curious without needing to "believe" or "disbelieve" yet.

## Intermediate Viewer

The intermediate viewer is ready for structured, school-labeled doctrine: the difference between Loan Đầu and Lý Khí, between Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh, between the two Bát Quái tables, and the structural anatomy of a Tử Vi lá số (12 Cung, 14 Chính Tinh, Cục).

They need:

- source and school references for every specific rule;
- repeated concept maps connecting Âm Dương/Ngũ Hành/Bát Quái to both tracks;
- comparison between popular misconception and structural fact;
- consistent reinforcement of the individualized-reading boundary at exactly the moments where it is most tempting to cross;
- careful, non-transactional application guidance.

## Advanced Viewer

The advanced viewer can handle historical debate, source investigation, cross-school comparison, and unresolved technical questions (e.g., the Cục numbering justification, the Kua-number lunar-conversion question, the Ngũ Hành attribution variance among the 14 Chính Tinh).

They need:

- interpretive humility;
- historical confidence labels applied consistently;
- deeper comparative context across Vietnamese and Chinese source material;
- explicit acknowledgment of open, unresolved technical questions rather than invented resolutions;
- long-form documentary structure for feature episodes.

## Transformation Map

1. Orientation: understanding what Tử Vi and Phong Thủy are and are not.
2. Safety: learning that the channel will never read the viewer's own chart or house.
3. Shared grammar: understanding Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, and Bát Quái as the common root.
4. Branch clarity: understanding Tử Vi's structure (Thiên Can, Địa Chi, 12 Cung, 14 Chính Tinh) and Phong Thủy's structure (Loan Đầu, Lý Khí) as related but distinct disciplines.
5. Plurality: understanding that schools can disagree, and that this is honest, not a flaw.
6. Historical humility: understanding which claims are structural consensus and which are legend.
7. Discernment: recognizing fear-sales patterns and financial/health overreach in commercial media.
8. Cultural respect: relating to inherited family practice with understanding rather than either anxiety or contempt.
9. Selective application: applying what is useful (a color choice, a spatial principle, a piece of vocabulary) without transactional expectation.
10. Maturity: holding open historical and cross-school questions with comfort rather than needing false resolution.

## Trust Map

Trust is built by:

- naming the school before stating a rule;
- naming uncertainty and legend as legend;
- never performing a personal reading, ever, even when directly requested in comments;
- never promising a guaranteed outcome;
- using consistent, glossary-controlled terminology;
- actively naming and refusing fear-sales patterns;
- showing visual restraint instead of mystical spectacle;
- correcting misconceptions without mockery;
- citing which Knowledge Packet and which source tier underlies a claim.

Trust is lost by:

- performing or simulating a reading for a viewer;
- "bạn sẽ..." certainty language;
- silently blending two independent schools into one invented formula;
- fear-based hooks or manufactured urgency;
- presenting legendary attribution as confirmed history;
- financial or health claims tied to a chart or layout;
- mocking family devotion or genuine practitioners;
- overproduced mystical spectacle that borrows horoscope-app or infomercial aesthetics.

## Knowledge Map

The knowledge map moves from simple to complex:

- Series identity, boundaries, and the individualized-reading rule.
- Shared foundation: Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành.
- Bát Quái and the Tiên Thiên/Hậu Thiên distinction.
- Tử Vi's calendrical grammar: Thiên Can, Địa Chi.
- Tử Vi's chart structure: 12 Cung, Cục, 14 Chính Tinh.
- Phong Thủy's Loan Đầu (form) foundation.
- Phong Thủy's Lý Khí foundation: Bát Trạch and Huyền Không Phi Tinh, and their disagreement.
- Applied home Phong Thủy: hướng nhà, bàn thờ, bếp, cửa chính.
- Vật phẩm literacy and anti-fear-sales discernment.
- Historical and legendary origins, tiered by confidence.
- Modern integration and selective application.

## Emotion Map

The emotion map moves from curiosity and safety into grounded discernment:

- curiosity;
- relief from fear-based framing;
- recognition (family memories, inherited practices);
- structural satisfaction (things "clicking" into a coherent map);
- mild productive suspense (historical mysteries, school disagreements);
- discernment (recognizing exploitation patterns);
- respect (for tradition and for family who practiced it);
- groundedness;
- selective confidence in applying what is useful.

The series should not trap viewers in either anxious belief or contemptuous disbelief. Even the most technically dense episodes must make room for cultural warmth, historical wonder, and practical calm.

# Season Architecture

## Season 1 - Nền Tảng Và Những Điều Thường Bị Hiểu Sai

**Mission:** Orient viewers to both Tử Vi and Phong Thủy, establish the shared Âm Dương/Ngũ Hành/Bát Quái/Khí grammar, and permanently establish the series' non-personalized, non-exploitative, source-aware lens before any deeper content is produced — while keeping every episode watchable on its own by giving it a real misconception to dismantle, not just a concept to define.

**Knowledge Focus:** Âm Dương, Ngũ Hành, Khí, Bát Quái, Thiên Can/Địa Chi, the 12 Cung, the 14 Chính Tinh, Loan Đầu, Bát Trạch vs. Huyền Không Phi Tinh, hướng nhà theo mệnh chủ, Cung Mệnh vs. Cung Thân, bàn thờ/bếp/cửa chính, vật phẩm phong thủy, a two-episode school-plurality-plus-case-study pair, and a closing integration episode.

**Emotional Arc:** Curiosity, orientation, safety (from both fear and mockery), structural satisfaction, discernment.

**Narrative Arc:** The viewer moves from encountering Tử Vi and Phong Thủy as fragmented cultural noise (a tỳ hưu here, a "hướng xấu" warning there, a relative's lá số elsewhere) to seeing both as structured, related, historically layered systems with clear internal logic and clear boundaries around what the channel will and will not do.

**Transformation Goal:** Viewers finish Season 1 with the full shared vocabulary, an accurate map of both tracks' basic structure, a firm, internalized understanding that this channel explains traditions and never reads a viewer's personal chart or house — and, episode by episode, one specific popular misconception corrected rather than a lecture absorbed.

**Structural Rule — One Central Misconception Per Episode (added 2026-07-17):** every Season 1 episode is built around a single named popular misconception (`hiểu lầm trung tâm`) it exists to dismantle. The episode's own working formula, binding for scripting: (1) state the popular belief, (2) explain why people believe it, (3) explain what the tradition actually says, (4) show how schools/sources differ where relevant, (5) show what is genuinely applicable, (6) name what must not be absolutized. This is a structural requirement, not a stylistic suggestion — an episode without a clear central misconception has not met this Series Bible's Season 1 spec. See `SEASON_01_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` for each episode's specific misconception.

**Rhythm Rule — Foundation Runs Are Bounded and Broken Up By Applied/Debate/Case-Study Beats (corrected 2026-07-20):** Season 1 uses eight rhythm labels, not four — **Mở đầu** (series/track opener), **Nền tảng** (concept explainer), **Ứng dụng** (home-life application, high visual/Shorts potential; Episode 13 is a hybrid Ứng dụng/Myth-bust beat), **Đào sâu** (a focused deep-dive distinction), **Tranh luận** (school-conflict/debate framing), **Case-study** (the whole toolkit applied to one illustrative example), and **Kết mùa** (season close). Two runs of three consecutive Nền tảng episodes exist by design — Ep002-004 (Âm Dương → Ngũ Hành → Khí, the shared cross-track grammar both tracks need before their first applied episode) and Ep010-012 (Bát Quái → Loan Đầu → 14 Chính Tinh, the last conceptual ground both tracks need before Episode 13's myth-bust and the Ep015-016 Tranh luận/Case-study pair) — each run is immediately followed by a change of mode: Ep004 gives way to Ep005's Mở đầu (opening the Tử Vi track) and then Ep006's Ứng dụng, while Ep012 gives way directly to Ep013's Ứng dụng/Myth-bust beat — so no run exceeds three and no viewer faces more than three purely conceptual episodes before the rhythm shifts. See the Episode Catalog below for each episode's assigned type.

**Expected Episode Count:** 17 episodes (fixed and binding — see `SEASON_01_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` for the exact topic assignment and full per-episode brief, which supersedes this document's Episode Catalog numbering for production purposes). This was revised from an earlier 15-episode draft after channel-owner review identified two real gaps: no dedicated episode defined "Khí" despite the term being load-bearing for Loan Đầu, hướng nhà, and Huyền Không content (now Episode 4), and no episode demonstrated the whole system against one concrete example (now Episode 16, paired with Episode 15's school-plurality lesson).

**Episode 16 Ground Rule (non-negotiable):** the case-study house used in Episode 16 must be entirely fictional or a composite built from generic patterns — never a real, identifiable address, building, or living family's home. It exists to demonstrate how Loan Đầu, Bát Trạch, and Huyền Không Phi Tinh can each produce a different, internally valid reading of the *same* floor plan — not to crown one school the winner. This is already covered by §4's existing "clearly fictional or historical/legendary example" allowance and does not require the newer §4a exception.

**Estimated Runtime:** 12-22 minutes per Nền tảng/Ứng dụng episode; 18-25 minutes for the Tranh luận/Case-study pair (Episodes 15-16); up to 28 minutes for the season-opening (Episode 1) and season-closing (Episode 17) integration episodes.

# Long-term Knowledge Roadmap

## What Viewers Learn First

Viewers must first learn how to approach these traditions safely and how the channel will behave. The first stage of knowledge is not technical mastery but orientation, boundary-setting, and trust. The opening episodes must establish that this is not a fortune-telling channel, not a fear-sales channel, and not a channel that will ever calculate a viewer's personal chart or floor plan.

First knowledge layer:

- the series lens and the individualized-reading boundary;
- Âm Dương and Ngũ Hành as shared root grammar;
- the difference between structural consensus and school-specific rule;
- the existence of multiple schools and lineages, named honestly;
- the difference between explaining a tradition and performing a reading;
- the anti-fear-sales standard, stated early and reinforced often.

Early episodes should repeat these foundational boundaries often enough for beginners to feel safe. This repetition must feel like protection, not padding.

## What Must Wait

The following topics must wait until viewers have sufficient structural and boundary context:

- star-by-star deep dives into the 14 Chính Tinh (Season 2);
- detailed Kua-number and Huyền Không Vận/Phi Tinh worked examples (Season 3, fictional-example-only);
- the historical debate arc — founding legends, competing attributions (Season 4);
- Cục numerological justification, which remains genuinely unresolved in current sources and may never fully "resolve" — this should be flagged as an open question indefinitely, not merely delayed;
- bàn thờ gia tiên content at high formality/sensitivity, pending an independent ethnographic source beyond the current Tier 3 commercial/folk material (see Known Gaps in `KP_FS_001`);
- deep cross-school comparative case studies (Season 3-4).

## What Must Never Fully "Graduate" Into Applied Claims

Unlike most Level-1-to-advanced knowledge in this roadmap, Cung Tật Ách and any lifespan/health/misfortune-timing content does not follow the normal pattern of "wait, then unlock." Per `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §8, this content must remain at the historical/structural level permanently, across every season, including the most advanced. Advanced seasons may go deeper into *why* the tradition includes this territory and *how* schools have historically softened it, but must never progress toward an applied personal or applied-fictional claim, at any point in the series' multi-year lifespan.

## Prerequisites

Before any episode is scripted, the assigned AI writer must know:

- the primary season;
- the selected episode concept and its exact wording per this catalog or `SEASON_01_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`;
- the required KP(s) and their current QA status;
- the school(s)/lineage(s) involved, and the requirement to name them explicitly;
- the confidence tier of every specific claim to be used;
- the canonical risk level and any escalated-caution flags (Cung Tật Ách, vật phẩm, historical attribution, etc.);
- the individualized-reading boundary reminder appropriate to the topic;
- CTA limits (no invitation to submit birth data or floor plans, no fear-based urgency).

If any prerequisite is missing, scripting must not begin.

## Advanced Topics

Advanced topics reserved for later seasons may include:

- doctrinal/technical detail of chính tinh placement mechanics (explicitly out of scope for `KP_FS_002` and reserved for a future dedicated packet);

Advanced episodes must be slower, more source-led, and more careful with boundaries than Season 1 content.
