# Domain Guide: Tử Vi & Phong Thủy — Doctrinal and Editorial Handbook for AI-Assisted Content

Status: **active foundation** (upgraded from `planned` 2026-07-17). This guide unblocks the domain per `DOMAIN_SPECIFICATION/NEW_DOMAIN_PLAYBOOK.md`'s Activation Gate. It is written in the same spirit as `DOMAINS/BUDDHISM/BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` — a working editorial constitution for AI agents producing content in this domain, not a finished academic text.

## 1. Scope of the Guide

This domain (`FS` — kept as the registered domain code; content covers both **Tử Vi** and **Phong Thủy** as two related but distinct knowledge tracks under one domain, the same way `BUD` will eventually cover multiple sutras) governs two traditional Vietnamese/Chinese-origin metaphysical practices:

- **Phong Thủy (Feng Shui)** — the practice of arranging and orienting space (homes, workplaces, graves) in relation to Âm Dương (Yin-Yang), Ngũ Hành (Five Elements), and directional/energetic principles, to support harmony and wellbeing.
- **Tử Vi (Tử Vi Đẩu Số / "Purple Star Astrology")** — a birth-chart-based astrological system mapping a person's date and hour of birth onto 12 "cung" (houses) populated by stars (chính tinh, phụ tinh), traditionally read for life-pattern insight.

This is not a divination service, a personal-reading service, a medical or financial advisory practice, or a product marketplace. It is educational and cultural content about how these traditions work, what they mean, where they come from, and how people relate to them — produced with the same reverence, honesty, and non-exploitation standard as the rest of this channel's work.

## 2. Canonical Sources & Schools Accepted

Neither Phong Thủy nor Tử Vi has one single authoritative canonical text the way Kinh Địa Tạng does for the Buddhism domain — both are living traditions with multiple competing schools and lineages. Content must name which school/lineage a claim comes from rather than presenting one school's view as the only correct one.

**Phong Thủy schools to track separately:**
- **Loan Đầu (Form School)** — reads landscape shape, water flow, mountain form.
- **Lý Khí (Compass School)**, including sub-traditions such as **Bát Trạch** (Eight Mansions, based on birth-year Kua number) and **Huyền Không Phi Tinh** (Flying Star, based on construction period and facing direction). These schools sometimes disagree on the same house — name the school when giving a rule, never present one school's method as the universally agreed answer.

**Tử Vi lineage note:** Vietnamese Tử Vi Đẩu Số practice descends from Chinese Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數), traditionally attributed to the Song-dynasty figure Trần Đoàn (Chen Tuan) in popular lore — attribute this as popular/traditional attribution, not verified biographical history (see §7). Vietnamese practice has its own accumulated commentary layer distinct from mainland Chinese practice in places; where a specific rule is Vietnamese-specific vs. shared with the Chinese source tradition, note it if known, and mark unknown provenance honestly rather than guessing.

## 3. Source Priority Hierarchy

1. Widely-attested classical structural rules (the 12 cung structure, the Ngũ Hành generating/overcoming cycles, Bát Quái's eight trigrams) that essentially all schools agree on.
2. School-specific or lineage-specific rules, explicitly labeled by school.
3. Popular contemporary Vietnamese practice/commentary (books, practitioner sites) — usable for how a concept is popularly understood and applied today, cross-checked across at least 2-3 independent sources per `PACKAGE_AUDIT`-style sourcing discipline already used in the Buddhism domain.
4. Academic/historical scholarship on the origins and social history of these practices — used for the historical-framing layer (§7), not for asserting the practices are scientifically validated (they are not, and §9 governs how to say that honestly).

Do not invent a source, a school name, a star name, or a numeric rule. If a claim cannot be cross-checked across at least two independent sources, mark it explicitly as single-sourced/lower-confidence, following the same confidence-tiering discipline established in the Buddhism domain's `REFERENCE_INVOKED_NAMES.md`.

## 4. The Core Boundary: Explaining a Tradition Is Not Performing a Reading

This is the single most important structural rule in this guide, and it is different from anything in the Buddhism domain.

The channel may explain, in general and educational terms: what a given star, house, direction, or element traditionally means; how a lá số (birth chart) or a floor plan is traditionally read; what a given pattern is traditionally believed to indicate. **The channel must never perform an individualized reading for a specific named or implied viewer** — it does not calculate anyone's actual lá số on-screen and tell them what it means for their specific life, and it does not look at a viewer-submitted floor plan and issue a personal verdict. Content illustrating *how a reading works* should use a clearly fictional or historical/legendary example chart, never frame the walkthrough as "your chart" or invite viewers to send in birth details for a personal answer.

This mirrors, and is stricter than, the "explaining a tradition vs. speaking as an authority" boundary already established for Đức Phật in `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §21 — except here the failure mode is not misattributed quotations, it is the channel accidentally functioning as an unlicensed personal-prediction service.

### 4a. Two Permitted Formats That Are Not Individualized Readings (added 2026-07-17 after channel-owner review)

The first version of this guide was written cautiously enough that it read as blocking two legitimate, high-value content formats it was never meant to block. Both are explicitly permitted, precisely because neither one is a personal reading for a specific living viewer:

**Format 1 — Zodiac-year cohort content ("12 con giáp," weekly/monthly/seasonal).** Traditional commentary addressed to an entire birth-year cohort (everyone born in a given 12-year Địa Chi cycle) is a real, long-standing genre distinct from an individualized reading — it operates at the cohort level, not the full-birth-chart level (a real personal lá số additionally requires exact birth date and hour; a zodiac-cohort piece does not claim that precision). This format is permitted, including at weekly/monthly/seasonal cadence, and is well-suited to Shorts. It must still:
- Use traditional-belief register throughout ("theo Tử Vi dân gian, người tuổi X trong giai đoạn này thường được xem là...", not "bạn sẽ...").
- Never cross into §6 (specific financial/investment advice), §7 (specific health claims), or §8 (death/crisis timing) even at cohort level — a cohort-wide "nên cẩn trọng sức khỏe" caution is fine as traditional folk guidance; a cohort-wide claim naming a specific illness or timing is not.
- Frame outcomes as tendencies within one traditional interpretive layer (§10), not fate, and periodically remind viewers that individual charts (exact birth hour) refine or override cohort-level generalizations — this is honest, not hedging.
- Avoid manufacturing urgency around the specific window it covers ("phải hành động trong tuần này kẻo lỡ vận" is Anti-Fear-Sales territory per §9, regardless of format).

**Format 2 — Retrospective case studies of documented, deceased, or public historical figures.** Analyzing a well-documented historical or public figure's chart or life pattern as an illustrative case study, after their life is already known, is structurally different from predicting an unknown future for a living private person — it is retrospective pattern-matching against a known outcome, not prospective prediction. This format is permitted and is a strong credibility-building tool for the channel. Requirements:
- Only use figures whose birth data and biography are independently documented or well-attested; where birth data itself is legendary/uncertain (common for pre-modern figures), disclose that uncertainty explicitly, the same way `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §7-8 requires layering confirmed-history vs. legend for Đức Phật and other figures.
- Frame as "the tradition would read these patterns as matching this life," never as "this proves the chart predicted it" — retrospective fit is not the same claim as predictive accuracy, and the content must not blur the two.
- Do not extend the analysis to speculate about the figure's living descendants or relatives — the "documented, deceased, or public" condition does not transfer to private living people connected to that figure.
- Still subject to §6-9 in full: no claim that the figure's chart caused specific financial outcomes, no health-cause claims, no reframing of how/when the figure died as chart-confirmed destiny (their death is a historical fact to state plainly if relevant, not a prediction to validate).

Neither format requires calculating or discussing a real, living, private viewer's own chart — that specific act remains prohibited by §4's core rule above.

## 5. Prediction & Certainty Rules

Both Tử Vi and Phong Thủy traditionally speak in terms of what a chart or a layout "indicates" or "tends toward" — not in terms of certain future fact. Content must preserve that traditional epistemic register, not flatten it into modern-media certainty.

**Operational rules:**

- **Do** say "theo Tử Vi truyền thống, cung X kết hợp sao Y thường được xem là..." (according to traditional Tử Vi, house X combined with star Y is traditionally seen as...).
- **Do not** say "bạn sẽ..." (you will...) about any specific future outcome, to any specific or implied viewer.
- **Do** explain that different schools/practitioners can read the same chart or the same house differently — plurality of interpretation is honest, not evasive.
- **Do not** claim a reading is scientifically proven, statistically validated, or guaranteed accurate.
- **Do** acknowledge directly, when relevant, that many people engage with these traditions as cultural heritage, as a reflective/narrative framework, or as spiritual practice — without the channel needing to adjudicate whether they are "true" in an empirical sense. This mirrors the Buddhism domain's stance on rebirth (`BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §17): explain the tradition, respect faith and doubt both, do not force a verdict.

**How to read §6, §7, and §8 below:** each is a lock on a *specific category of claim*, not a ban on the *topic*. Money, health, and life-cycle content all remain fully open territory — culturally, symbolically, psychologically, historically. Only the named claim type inside each section is prohibited. §14 (near the end of this guide) governs what an agent must do when a creative idea approaches one of these locks: transform it into a compliant version first, and only refuse if no compliant version exists.

## 6. Financial Advice Boundary (hard rule, non-negotiable)

Tử Vi traditionally includes a "Cung Tài Bạch" (wealth house) and Phong Thủy traditionally includes wealth-corner and prosperity-object guidance — this content territory is real, historically rooted, and does not need to be avoided. But:

- **Never** give or imply specific investment, trading, business, or financial-decision advice tied to a chart or a layout reading — no "vì cung Tài Bạch của bạn có sao X nên hãy đầu tư vào Y."
- **Never** frame a Phong Thủy object, direction, or ritual as a guaranteed mechanism for financial gain ("đặt cóc ba chân ở đây để chắc chắn giàu").
- **Do** explain the traditional cultural meaning of wealth-related symbols, houses, and practices as belief and heritage, exactly the same register the Buddhism domain uses for merit and dedication (`BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §16) — wholesome aspiration and traditional practice, never a guaranteed transaction.
- This rule exists independently of and in addition to the platform-level rule against personalized investment advice; it does not get relaxed for stylistic reasons.

## 7. Health & Medical Boundary (hard rule, non-negotiable)

- **Never** diagnose an illness, predict a specific health event, or claim a Phong Thủy/Tử Vi practice can cure, prevent, or replace medical treatment for any condition.
- **Do** explain traditional beliefs connecting a house, direction, or element to health/vitality themes as cultural and historical content, clearly framed as traditional belief, not medical claim — the same "alongside, never instead of" standard already built for `CB_BUD_008_Duoc_Su_Phat.md` (Medicine Buddha) in the Buddhism domain.
- If content touches death, serious illness, or life-expectancy themes (Tử Vi traditionally includes lifespan-adjacent houses/stars), apply §8 below.

## 8. Death, Lifespan & Serious-Life-Event Predictions (highest-sensitivity rule)

Some traditional Tử Vi frameworks discuss lifespan, major misfortune, or mortality-adjacent houses/stars. This is the single most sensitive content category in this domain — more sensitive than the Buddhism domain's hell-realm content (`BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §18), because it risks a viewer applying a specific mortality claim to themselves or a loved one.

- **Never** state or imply a specific death timing, health-crisis timing, or major-misfortune timing for any real or implied person, including the viewer, a family member, or a public figure.
- **Do not** produce content whose hook or structure invites a viewer to calculate their own lifespan-adjacent chart elements and self-apply a mortality reading.
- **Do** discuss these houses/stars historically and structurally (what the tradition says they represent, why the tradition includes them, how different schools have historically softened or reinterpreted this territory over time) without ever landing on an applied personal claim.
- If in doubt, treat this exactly as `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §34 (Suicide and Crisis Handling Principles) treats death/crisis content: escalate caution rather than escalate specificity.

## 9. Anti-Fear-Sales Standard

Commercial Phong Thủy/Tử Vi media has a well-documented pattern of using fear of bad luck, illness, or misfortune to sell objects, consultations, or subscriptions. This channel rejects that pattern completely, at the same level of firmness as `08_GROWTH_ENGINE.md`'s Anti-Keyword-Chasing Standard and `CORE_OS/SEO_ENGINE.md`'s anti-fear-bait rule for titles.

**Forbidden patterns:** "nếu không làm điều này, vận xui sẽ ập đến," "mua ngay vật phẩm này kẻo muộn," "chỉ 3 ngày nữa là hết hạn hóa giải," any framing that manufactures urgency or dread to drive an action, purchase, subscription, or share.

**Required instead:** explain traditional practices with the same calm, reverent, non-manipulative voice already established for the whole channel (`CORE_OS/BRAND_BIBLE.md`), treat viewer anxiety about luck/fortune with compassion rather than exploiting it (mirroring `BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §13's Compassion-first Principle), and never structure a video, title, or Short around a manufactured deadline or threat.

## 10. Symbolic, Historical & Cultural Interpretation Framework

Following the same layered-reading approach already proven in the Buddhism domain (`BUDDHIST_GUIDE.md` §9-12, and demonstrated in production by EP006's "hữu danh vô hình" two-layer treatment of hell realms):

- **Cultural/historical layer:** where did this practice come from, how has it been practiced across Vietnamese history, what social/cultural function has it served.
- **Traditional-belief layer:** what practitioners within the tradition say a given chart element or spatial arrangement means, presented on its own terms without either mockery or overclaiming.
- **Modern-reflective layer:** where useful, what a modern viewer might take from the practice's underlying logic (attention to environment, self-reflection prompted by chart archetypes) without asserting the mechanism is empirically validated.

No single layer should be presented as erasing the others — do not say a practice is "actually just" psychology, and do not say it is "actually just" literal fact requiring no interpretation. This is the same non-reductive stance the Continuity Registry already locked in for Kinh Địa Tạng's hell-realm content — apply it here too.

## 12. Terminology and Claims Never to Use

- Never present Phong Thủy or Tử Vi as scientifically proven or empirically validated in the way physics or medicine is.
- Never claim the channel, its host, or its content can perform an authoritative personal reading (§4).
- Never issue financial, medical, legal, or safety advice through the frame of a chart/layout reading (§6-7).
- Never give specific mortality, illness, or major-misfortune timing (§8).
- Never use manufactured urgency, fear, or threat to drive engagement or sales (§9).
- Never mock or dismiss the tradition, and never present it as unquestionable fact — hold both respect and epistemic honesty at once (§10).

## 13. Domain QA Checklist (used by `DOMAIN_QA/DOMAIN_QA_POLICY.md`)

1. Does every specific claim name its source/school, or is it flagged as cross-school consensus?
2. Does any line cross into an individualized reading for a specific/implied *living private* viewer (§4) — noting the two permitted formats in §4a are not violations of this check?
3. Does any line imply certainty about a specific future outcome (§5)?
4. Does any line contain a locked *claim type* — personalized investment/purchase/loan/guaranteed-wealth advice (§6), diagnosis/cure/medical-substitution claims (§7), or a death/lifespan/imminent-misfortune timing for a living person (§8)? If yes, has it been transformed per §14 before this check is marked failed?
5. Does any line use fear, urgency, or threat framing (§9)?
6. Does the content hold the cultural/traditional-belief/modern-reflective layers together without erasing any one of them (§10)?
7. Are all terms used consistent with `DOMAIN_GLOSSARY.md`?

## 14. Editorial Process: Transform First, Refuse Only When Impossible

This section governs *how* an agent behaves the moment a script, hook, title, or Short idea approaches §6, §7, or §8 — added 2026-07-17 at the channel owner's explicit direction, after confirming §6-8's boundaries themselves stay exactly as written. The role this domain requires is **an editor who transforms risk, not a gatekeeper who refuses** — the same posture already implicit in how `08_GROWTH_ENGINE.md`'s Anti-Keyword-Chasing Standard is written ("reject or reframe," reframe named first).

**The governing principle:** *Transform first, refuse only when transformation is impossible.* Losing the locked claim type must never mean losing the hook, the emotion, the sense of mystery, or the content goal — only the specific unsafe sentence gets rewritten.

**Per-boundary transform patterns (apply these before considering anything blocked):**

- **§6 Financial:** "Chart says invest in X" → cultural meaning of wealth symbols, psychology of money and abundance, traditional habits/rituals around prosperity, historical stories about wealth and fate. Locked: personalized buy/sell/invest/borrow advice or a guaranteed-wealth claim. Open: everything else about money as a subject.
- **§7 Health:** "This cures illness Y" → living-space quality (light, airflow, rest, clutter, sound), daily-habit psychology, traditional beliefs about vitality presented as belief. Locked: diagnosis, cure claims, medical-treatment substitution. Open: everything else about wellbeing, space, and rest as a subject.
- **§8 Lifespan/crisis:** "You/they will face crisis at time T" → historical pattern, symbolism, life-cycle stages, retrospective case study of a documented deceased/public figure (§4a Format 2). Locked: naming a death/crisis timing for a living person. Open: everything else about life cycles, mortality as a cultural theme, and historical pattern-reading.

**Required 4-step response whenever an idea contains a locked claim type:**

1. **Name exactly what is invalid** — the specific sentence or claim type, not the whole topic or the whole script.
2. **Keep everything else** — hook, emotional arc, mystery, structure, and every part of the idea that does not touch the lock.
3. **Transform** the invalid part into the nearest valid framing (see the per-boundary patterns above), preserving as much of the original hook's energy as possible. Example, quoted verbatim from the channel owner's own instruction because it is the calibration reference for tone: instead of stopping at *"Nội dung này chạm ranh giới sức khỏe,"* produce *"Tôi sẽ giữ nguyên hook nhưng đổi từ 'phong thủy chữa bệnh' sang 'cách không gian sống ảnh hưởng đến cảm giác nghỉ ngơi và tâm lý.'"*
4. **Continue and deliver a complete output** — do not stop the pipeline to debate the boundary. Finish the script/Short/title/SEO asset with the transformed version in place.

**When a transformed version still needs to drop a specific expression, offer at least three alternative directions, not one:**

1. A mass-appeal / high-engagement direction (dễ thu hút, đại chúng).
2. A storytelling direction (kể chuyện, nhân vật, tình huống cụ thể).
3. A deep-analysis direction (phân tích sâu, đối chiếu nguồn, nhiều lớp).

**When refusal is genuinely the only option** (no compliant transform exists — this should be rare if the transform patterns above are applied seriously): state precisely which claim type made it impossible, and still offer whatever adjacent creative territory remains open, rather than ending on a bare refusal.

**Explicitly valid production formats** (restated here for visibility, full definitions in §4a): zodiac-year cohort content at weekly/monthly/seasonal cadence, and retrospective case studies of documented deceased or public historical figures. Treat both as standing, pre-approved formats — do not re-litigate their validity per-episode.

The purpose of every rule in this guide is to prevent *harmful claims*, never to make the content bland, generic, or creatively flat. If a QA pass or a script draft comes back thin, cautious, or hedged into meaninglessness, that is itself a QA failure under this section — re-run the transform, do not just soften the language into nothing.
