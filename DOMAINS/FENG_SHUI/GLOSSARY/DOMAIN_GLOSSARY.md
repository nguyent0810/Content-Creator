## A. Shared Cosmological Foundation (used by both tracks)

**Âm Dương (Yin-Yang)** — Two opposing, complementary forces whose interplay is believed to underlie all phenomena; rooted in Kinh Dịch (I Ching) philosophy. Used in Phong Thủy for spatial balance and in Tử Vi for the qualitative character of stars/houses. *Rule:* present as a traditional philosophical framework, not an empirically measured force (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §10).

**Ngũ Hành (Five Elements — Kim/Mộc/Thủy/Hỏa/Thổ)** — Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth; the generative and destructive relationships between them govern much of both Phong Thủy and Tử Vi reasoning.
- **Tương Sinh (generating cycle):** Mộc sinh Hỏa → Hỏa sinh Thổ → Thổ sinh Kim → Kim sinh Thủy → Thủy sinh Mộc (loop). Verified high-confidence, unanimous across sources.
- **Tương Khắc (overcoming cycle):** Mộc khắc Thổ → Thổ khắc Thủy → Thủy khắc Hỏa → Hỏa khắc Kim → Kim khắc Mộc (loop). Verified high-confidence, unanimous across sources.
*Rule:* these two cycles are the one piece of near-universal cross-school agreement in this domain — safe to state as settled fact, unlike most other claims here, which need school attribution.

**Khí (Qi/Chi)** — A traditional Chinese philosophical category ("breath," "vital force," the animating principle of change) predating and broader than Phong Thủy, later borrowed into site-selection reasoning; in Phong Thủy it describes how a landscape or dwelling is felt to gather ("tụ," Sinh khí) or scatter ("tán," Tà khí/Sát khí) an invisible flow, read differently by each school — statically via terrain (Loan Đầu), statically via the occupant's Quái Mệnh (Bát Trạch), or dynamically via construction-period/facing (Huyền Không Phi Tinh); do not merge these three into one mechanism. Its closest modern-reflective analogy is natural ventilation/light, but that is an analogy, not a scientific equivalence. *Rule:* never state or imply Khí is a measured physical energy (electromagnetic field, thermodynamic quantity, or any instrument-detectable quantity) — this is the domain's single most important never-cross line (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §12; full depth and script-ready material in `KNOWLEDGE_PACKETS/KP_FS_001_Phong_Thuy.md`'s dedicated Khí section; this is SB_FS_001 Episode 4's central misconception).

## B. Phong Thủy Terms

**Bát Quái (Eight Trigrams)** — Càn, Khảm, Cấn, Chấn, Tốn, Ly, Khôn, Đoài; each linked to a Ngũ Hành element and a direction. Two distinct arrangements exist — **Tiên Thiên Bát Quái** (Earlier Heaven) and **Hậu Thiên Bát Quái** (Later Heaven) — do not conflate them; exact directional placement of Tiên Thiên has minor cross-source disagreement, flag as such rather than asserting one arrangement with full confidence.

**Loan Đầu (Form School)** — Reads landscape shape, water flow, and terrain (long mạch/dragon vein, minh đường/bright hall, Tứ Tượng — Thanh Long/Bạch Hổ/Chu Tước/Huyền Vũ as the four cardinal-direction guardian animals). A distinct method from the compass-based schools below — do not merge its logic with Lý Khí methods as if they were one system.

**Lý Khí (Compass School)** — The umbrella term for direction/time-based methods, including the two below. *Rule:* always specify which Lý Khí method (Bát Trạch or Huyền Không Phi Tinh) a claim comes from — they can disagree on the same house (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2).

**Bát Trạch (Eight Mansions)** — A Tam Hợp-based method computing a person's Quái Mệnh (Kua number) from their birth year, dividing houses into Đông Tứ Trạch (East Four Houses) / Tây Tứ Trạch (West Four Houses) groups with associated favorable/unfavorable directions.

**Huyền Không Phi Tinh (Flying Star)** — A Tam Nguyên-based method computing a chart from a building's construction period (vận) and facing direction, tracking "flying stars" (phi tinh) across the floor plan. Genuinely different computational basis from Bát Trạch — the two can yield different verdicts on the same house; this is not an error in either system, it is two different traditions with different premises (`DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §2, confirmed via `RESEARCH_DRAFT_BAT_QUAI_TRUONG_PHAI.md`).

**Hướng nhà (house facing/orientation)** — The traditional practice of choosing or evaluating a building's direction, typically via Bát Trạch and/or Huyền Không Phi Tinh method (name which one is being applied).

**Trạch mệnh** — The pairing of a person's Mệnh (elemental/destiny category, from birth data) with a dwelling's Trạch (house category) — the conceptual basis for "matching a house to a person," central to Bát Trạch-style reasoning.

## C. Tử Vi Terms

**Tử Vi Đẩu Số** — The full name of the birth-chart astrological system this track covers; "Tử Vi" names its central star, "Đẩu Số" ("dipper/star numbers") reflects its star-based calculation method. *Rule:* earliest confirmed written use of this exact term dates to 1581 (Ming dynasty) — do not assert an earlier confirmed date without a source.

**Thiên Can (10 Heavenly Stems)** — Giáp, Ất, Bính, Đinh, Mậu, Kỷ, Canh, Tân, Nhâm, Quý — each carries an Âm Dương polarity and Ngũ Hành association, combined with Địa Chi to mark years/dates in the traditional calendar system underlying chart construction.

**Địa Chi (12 Earthly Branches)** — Tý, Sửu, Dần, Mão, Thìn, Tỵ, Ngọ, Mùi, Thân, Dậu, Tuất, Hợi — correspond to the 12 zodiac animals and mark years/months/days/hours in the lunar calendar system a Tử Vi chart is built from.

**Cung (House)** — One of 12 fixed positions in a Tử Vi chart, each governing a life domain. The 12 cung: **Mệnh** (destiny/core self), **Phụ Mẫu** (parents), **Phúc Đức** (blessings/fortune), **Điền Trạch** (property), **Quan Lộc** (career, also called Sự Nghiệp), **Nô Bộc** (relationships with subordinates/associates, also called Giao Hữu), **Thiên Di** (travel/movement), **Tật Ách** (health — see the highest-sensitivity rule below), **Tài Bạch** (wealth), **Tử Tức** (children, also called Tử Nữ), **Phu Thê** (spouse/partnership), **Huynh Đệ** (siblings). *Rule:* **Cung Tật Ách carries the domain's highest sensitivity rating** — any content touching it must follow `DOMAIN_GUIDE.md` §7-8 (health and lifespan boundaries) without exception.

**Chính tinh (Major Stars)** — The 14 principal stars placed across a chart's 12 cung: Tử Vi, Thiên Cơ, Thái Dương, Vũ Khúc, Thiên Đồng, Liêm Trinh, Thiên Phủ, Thái Âm, Tham Lang, Cự Môn, Thiên Tướng, Thiên Lương, Thất Sát, Phá Quân. *Rule:* 5 of these 14 (Thiên Cơ, Thiên Đồng, Thiên Tướng, Thất Sát, Thiên Lương) have cross-source disagreement on their Ngũ Hành association — present as school-dependent, not settled fact, for those five specifically.

**An sao (star placement)** — The technical process of calculating where each star falls in a specific chart, based on birth date/hour. *Rule:* detailed computational rules for this are currently low-confidence in this project's source base (`SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` gap) — do not present exact placement algorithms as verified until a stronger source is added; safe to explain the *concept* (why placement depends on birth data) without walking through real computation.

**Trần Đoàn** — The Song-dynasty figure popularly credited as Tử Vi Đẩu Số's founder. *Rule:* this is a legendary/traditional attribution, explicitly flagged as historically unverifiable even by mainstream tertiary sources — never state as confirmed biographical fact; a competing legend credits Lã Động Tân instead. Always use attribution language ("theo truyền thuyết phổ biến," "được cho là") never assertion language.

**Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang** — A 20th-century Vietnamese author associated with a widely-referenced Tử Vi text; minor biographical details vary across sources — verify further before using specific biographical facts in a script.
