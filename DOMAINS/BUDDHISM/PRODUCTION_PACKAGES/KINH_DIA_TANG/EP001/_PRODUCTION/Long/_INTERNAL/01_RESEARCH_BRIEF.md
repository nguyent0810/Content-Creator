## Core Research Question

Ai là Địa Tạng Bồ Tát trong bản kinh này — không phải như một vị thần quản lý cõi âm mà người hiện đại thường mặc định, mà như một vị Bồ Tát được nhận diện qua danh hiệu, biểu tượng và trên hết là một đại nguyện cụ thể? Tập này phải đưa người xem đi từ chỗ "nhận biết cái tên Địa Tạng" đến chỗ "hiểu được lời nguyện định nghĩa Ngài là ai."

## Viewer Transformation (per Episode Catalog)

**From:** name recognition — người xem biết cái tên "Địa Tạng Bồ Tát", có thể đã thấy tượng, đã nghe nhắc trong đám tang, nhưng không hiểu Ngài thực sự là ai trong giáo lý.

**To:** vow understanding — người xem hiểu rằng danh tính của Địa Tạng không nằm ở quyền năng hay vị trí trong vũ trụ quan dân gian, mà nằm ở đại nguyện: một cam kết đạo đức hình thành từ hiếu hạnh và mở rộng thành từ bi phổ quát.

Suggested Narrative Pattern: **NP071 — Vow Formation** (wound, awakening, compassion, declaration, endurance; "what makes someone vow never to abandon others").
Suggested Episode Blueprint: **BP028 — Bodhisattva Vow Episode** (cold open on suffering that calls forth vow → context on bodhisattva framing → vow formation/testing narrative → doctrinal boundary layer → tender emotional layer → small daily-vow application → benediction-style close).

- **Past-life / filial origin reference:** `kinh-dia-tang-1.txt`, lines 43–45 — describes a past life as a daughter of Brahman lineage ("thiếu nữ thuộc dòng dõi Bà La Môn") whose grief for her mother's suffering widens into a vow to save all beings; cross-check against CB_BUD_001's approved "Elder's Daughter" and "Brahman Woman" narrative categories before naming specific figures on screen.
- **Vow-as-non-abandonment, tested against the option of personal liberation:** `kinh-dia-tang-3.txt`, lines 9–13 — narrates the bodhisattva reaching the threshold of Buddhahood/Nirvana and choosing to turn back toward suffering beings rather than enter it alone; strong material for the NP071 "vow formation" emotional arc, usable as paraphrased narrative framing (not verbatim scripture).
- **"Vương" honorific caution:** CB_BUD_001 line 79 — "Vương" is common in devotional usage but must not imply worldly kingship or identity with Diêm La Vương.

## Canonical Boundaries To Respect (from SB_BUD_001)

- **No hell sensationalism:** this episode is about identity and vow, not hell-realm description; if hell is referenced at all, it must stay in the register "compassion directed toward beings in extreme suffering," never graphic or punitive (Series Tone — Forbidden Styles; KP_BUD_001 Hell Realm Rule).
- **No over-sanctification / no power-fantasy:** avoid superhero framing, magical staff/jewel powers, or guaranteed-rescue language (BUDDHIST_GUIDE §20 Bodhisattva Interpretation Rules; CB_BUD_001 Forbidden vow framing).
- **Respect tradition diversity:** do not claim Địa Tạng is worshipped identically across all Buddhist traditions; explicitly note Theravada does not treat this figure/text as Pali-canonical, and Japanese/Korean receptions differ (SB_BUD_001 Canonical Boundaries — Tradition Boundaries; CB_BUD_001 Tradition Differences).
- **Historical confidence labeling:** any claim about the sutra's textual history, canonical cataloging, or the bodhisattva's "biography" must be labeled by confidence tier (high/medium/low) per SB_BUD_001 Canonical Boundaries — Historical Confidence.
- **No invented dialogue or biography:** do not write fictional speech for Địa Tạng or invent biographical details not found in an approved source (BUDDHIST_GUIDE §20; CB_BUD_001 Teaching Style).
- **Filial piety without coercion:** since past-life narratives are filial in origin, any filial-piety framing must follow KP_BUD_001's Filial Piety Rule — gratitude and care, never blind obedience, never used to pressure viewers with harmful family situations (this episode should note the theme briefly since EP004 already covers it in depth — avoid duplicating that episode's core argument).

## Things Not To Claim

- Do not claim Địa Tạng is a god, judge of hell, or ruler of the dead.
- Do not claim the vow guarantees automatic rescue for any specific being.
- Do not claim the "Địa ngục vị không..." line is a verified verbatim quotation from an approved published sutra edition.
- Do not name past-life figures (e.g., "Mục Kiền Liên Thanh") without verification against CB_BUD_001's approved narrative categories.
- Do not claim the jewel grants worldly wealth or the staff functions as a weapon.
- Do not claim Địa Tạng devotion, iconography, or ritual practice is identical across Chinese, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, and Tibetan traditions.
- Do not claim historical biographical facts (birthplace, dates, lifetime) for Địa Tạng as a literal human figure.

## Suggested Emotional Hook

Per PROJECT_PRD.md (Vision, Mission, Target Audience §7), the channel's core audience includes Vietnamese-speaking viewers with inherited but under-explained temple/family practice, adults carrying grief or guilt, and viewers tired of fear-based or transactional spiritual media. The channel's brand promise is "depth without confusion, beauty without manipulation... reverence without superstition."

For this episode, the recommended emotional hook (consistent with NP071 Vow Formation) is:

**"The figure you thought you knew, and the vow you never heard."** Open on the familiar image most Vietnamese viewers already carry — a statue with staff and jewel, seen at a funeral or temple, associated vaguely with death and fear — then gently dismantle the misconception (not a god of hell) and rebuild it around a single human-scale question: what makes a vow strong enough to never turn away? This lets the episode do double duty: correcting a widespread misidentification (intellectual payoff) while landing the NP071 emotional arc of grief-into-commitment (emotional payoff) — without borrowing EP004's filial-piety-for-parents territory, since here the filial story is used only as the origin of the vow, not as the episode's main teaching.

## Research Confidence Summary

The episode is safe for a production draft once it treats the vow line as a traditional formulation (not a hard scriptural quote), avoids naming unverified past-life figures (notably the "Mục Kiền Liên Thanh" error found in the local source), keeps staff/jewel symbolism within CB_BUD_001's approved meanings, and opens by directly correcting the "god of the underworld" misconception. Human review is recommended for: past-life figure names before they appear on screen, and confirmation that this episode's filial-origin content stays subordinate to EP004's material rather than duplicating it.
