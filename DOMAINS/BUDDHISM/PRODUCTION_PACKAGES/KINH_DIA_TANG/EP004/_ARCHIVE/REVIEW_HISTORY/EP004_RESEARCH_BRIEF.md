## Research Objective

Create a safe evidence plan for Episode 4 by distinguishing canonical text, traditional interpretation, historical or cultural context, modern reflection, and creative framing around Cung Trời Đao Lợi and hiếu đạo.

## Episode Scope

Allowed scope:

- The Buddhist narrative setting of Cung Trời Đao Lợi / Trayastrimsa Heaven.
- The repository-supported connection between Đức Phật, his mother, and the opening setting.
- The editorial distinction between sutra narrative, devotional/traditional interpretation, modern reflection, and creative framing.
- Hiếu đạo as gratitude, remembrance, responsibility, care, and ethical transformation.

Out of scope:

- Finished script, narration, hook, scene text, or thumbnail copy.
- Unsourced scripture quotation.
- Scientific proof claims about Buddhist cosmology.
- Claims of guaranteed rescue, merit, health, wealth, or family outcome.
- Guilt-based filial piety or absolute obedience.

## Historical and Cultural Context

The Đao Lợi setting may be discussed as part of Buddhist cosmology and sutra narrative. It must not be presented as a modern scientific or secular historical claim. Historical development, translation history, and Vietnamese reception require additional sources before precise claims.

## Doctrinal Context

Use Mahayana scope. Avoid claiming all Buddhist traditions teach or emphasize Kinh Địa Tạng in the same way. Hiếu đạo must be framed with compassion and responsibility, not coercion.

## Interpretive Boundaries

| Layer | Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical layer | Source-backed setting and characters | Invented quotes or exact wording without verification |
| Commentarial layer | Labeled tradition/commentary | Commentary presented as sutra text |
| Historical layer | Verified historical context | Heavenly assembly as secular historical fact |
| Modern application | Family reflection, gratitude, repair | Guilt, shame, obedience absolutism |
| Creative framing | Mood, pacing, symbolic imagery | New doctrine or invented sacred events |

## Claims Requiring Direct Evidence

- Exact location of the Đao Lợi assembly in the source.
- Exact wording connecting Đức Phật's teaching and his mother.
- Exact basis for hiếu đạo in the text.
- Exact attendee details.

## Claims Requiring Attribution

- Kinh Địa Tạng is traditionally associated with hiếu đạo.
- Đao Lợi belongs to Buddhist cosmology.
- Modern filial reflection is an application of the theme, not the text itself.

## Claims That Must Not Be Made

- Đao Lợi is scientifically verified.
- Watching, chanting, donating, or a single ritual guarantees parental rescue or fortune.
- Hiếu đạo means absolute obedience.
- Parents suffer because the viewer failed a devotional act.
- Địa Tạng cancels karmic consequences mechanically.
