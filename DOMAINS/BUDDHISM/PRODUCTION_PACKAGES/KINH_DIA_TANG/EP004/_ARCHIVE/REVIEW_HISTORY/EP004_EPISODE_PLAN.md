## Audience Promise

The episode will help viewers understand why the Đao Lợi setting matters, how the Buddha-mother relationship is used in the Kinh Địa Tạng tradition, and how hiếu đạo can be approached with gratitude and transformation rather than guilt.

## Central Question

What does the Đao Lợi opening of Kinh Địa Tạng allow the channel to teach about filial gratitude without turning Buddhist cosmology, devotion, or family ethics into superstition or coercion?

## Episode Thesis

Working thesis for planning only: In the Kinh Địa Tạng tradition, the Đao Lợi assembly creates a sacred narrative frame where filial gratitude becomes more than family obligation; it becomes an ethical doorway into remembrance, transformation, and compassion for all beings. This thesis is conditional until exact canonical citation and commentarial attribution are verified.

## Character Usage

Use Đức Phật, Ma-da phu nhân, Địa Tạng Bồ Tát, chư thiên, and đại chúng only to the degree supported by source and assets. Do not invent speech, facial expressions, private feelings, or motivations.

## Creative Framing Boundaries

Creative framing may ask why the scene matters for modern viewers. It may not invent canonical scenes or present modern family pressure as stated sutra content.

## Modern Application Boundaries

Modern application must be framed as reflection, not doctrine. It must protect viewers in abusive or unsafe family contexts by avoiding absolute obedience language.

## Transition Logic

Transitions must move from source context to interpretation to application. No transition may smuggle an unsourced claim.
