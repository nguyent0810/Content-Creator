## Mandatory Visual Boundaries

- Do not present Buddhist cosmology as photographic history.
- Do not claim a single art style is the canonical form of Đao Lợi or Địa Tạng.
- Do not mix symbols from unrelated traditions without attribution.
- Do not create visuals that make creative imagination look like scripture.
- Do not use flames, horror, or punishment imagery for retention.
- Do not use filial-piety visuals that shame viewers or imply parental suffering is their fault.
