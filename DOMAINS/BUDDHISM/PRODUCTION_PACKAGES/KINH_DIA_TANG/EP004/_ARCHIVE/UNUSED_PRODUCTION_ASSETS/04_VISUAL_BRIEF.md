# 04 Visual Brief

## Visual Direction

The visual direction should feel cinematic, reverent, domestic, and luminous. The episode moves between two worlds: the sacred cosmological setting of Cung Trời Đao Lợi and the quiet Vietnamese home where filial love is tested in ordinary acts. The visuals must not turn Buddhist cosmology into documentary realism or fantasy spectacle.

## Primary Motif

The empty chair.

Use it as a visual anchor at the beginning, mid-video and end. It may be a wooden chair near a dining table, a chair beside an altar, or a chair by a window. It should never be melodramatic. It should feel like a place someone used to sit.

## Secondary Motifs

- Warm lamp or oil light.
- A bowl of rice.
- Old hands.
- A phone screen with an unanswered call or transfer notification, shown without readable private data.
- A folded towel or medicine box.
- Incense smoke used sparingly and respectfully.

## Scene Progression

1. Modern home, empty chair, late afternoon light.
2. Subtle symbolic transition into clouds, temple bells and vast light for Đao Lợi.
3. Sacred assembly suggested through silhouettes, light and space, not literal overdetail.
4. Return to real homes: transfer receipt, caregiving routine, family meal, hospital corridor.
5. Boundary-aware imagery: a closed door, a hand resting on a phone without calling, walking away calmly.
6. Generational healing: parent pausing before anger, child sleeping, a lamp remaining lit.
7. Final return to empty chair, now less accusatory and more contemplative.

## Images To Use

- Vietnamese family interiors with restraint.
- Warm, human close-ups.
- Temple bells, lamps, hands and quiet domestic objects.
- Soft symbolic heavenly light for Đao Lợi.
- Địa Tạng imagery only if dignified, compassionate and consistent with Character Bible.

## Images Not To Use

- Horror hell visuals.
- Demonic faces, torture, jump-scare flames or fear thumbnails.
- “Exact reconstruction” of Đao Lợi.
- Western angel imagery for chư thiên.
- Overly sentimental mother crying in staged melodrama.
- Images implying unsafe reconciliation.
- Images implying ritual guarantees.

## Visual Rhythm

The opening should be still and intimate. The Đao Lợi section should widen the frame. The modern reflection should return to close-ups. The emotional peak should slow down instead of intensifying with spectacle. The ending should be spare.

## Color

Use warm wood, muted gold, soft white, gentle gray, deep brown and restrained amber. Avoid a one-note gold fantasy palette. Do not make the entire episode dark or funeral-like.

## Lighting

Domestic scenes: late afternoon, window light, table lamp, altar lamp. Sacred scenes: diffuse gold-white light, soft clouds, no hard fantasy beams. Emotional boundary scenes: cooler but not bleak.

## Symbol Handling

Cung Trời Đao Lợi is a Buddhist cosmological and devotional setting. Visuals should be marked in style as symbolic. Do not imply that the studio knows the literal architecture of a heavenly realm.

## Thumbnail Concept

Recommended concept: a warm empty chair in the foreground, soft heavenly light in the background, and a dignified small silhouette of the Buddha teaching or a temple bell motif. Avoid death-bed shock imagery.

Suggested thumbnail text: `ĐỪNG ĐỢI GHẾ TRỐNG`
