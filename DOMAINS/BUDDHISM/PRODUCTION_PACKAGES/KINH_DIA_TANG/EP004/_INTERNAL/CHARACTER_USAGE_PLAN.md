## Character Control Rules

- No invented dialogue.
- No invented inner monologue.
- No motive beyond what the source or accepted tradition supports.
- Địa Tạng Bồ Tát cannot be turned into a fear figure or transaction engine.
- Đức Phật's filial action must not be psychologized beyond source support.
- Ma-da phu nhân must not be used as emotional bait.
