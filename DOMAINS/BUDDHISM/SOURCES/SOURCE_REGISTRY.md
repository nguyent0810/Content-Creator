## Description correction (2026 research pass)

`kinh-dia-tang-1.txt`, `kinh-dia-tang-2.txt`, and `kinh-dia-tang-3.txt` are **modern narration/reflection scripts already produced in-house**, not a scriptural translation and not a Tier 1 or Tier 2 source. Cross-chapter research (see RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH03_04_13.md) confirms these files contain: (a) an extended reflection on the meaning of "Dia" (earth) and "Tang" (treasury/store), (b) narration of the traditional sixteen-character vow formulation ("Dia nguc vi khong, the bat thanh Phat. Chung sinh do tan, phuong chung Bo De"), and (c) a short, imprecise, and partly erroneous reference to a past-life figure ("Muc Kien Lien Thanh") that does not correspond to any name in the actual sutra (see Continuity Registry). These files do **not** cover the specific content of Chapters 3, 4, 5, or 13 (hell names, the karma-retribution tables, the Quang Muc story, or the closing entrustment), and must not be treated as a source for those chapters' specific details, character names, or numeric claims. Reclassify these three files as **Tier 6 (Modern reflection, restricted)** — usable as narration-voice reference and as the source of the vow-line wording (itself unverified as verbatim scripture), but not as a citation source for scriptural facts, chapter content, or character names. Any future use of these files for scriptural claims requires cross-verification against a Tier 1/2 source first.

All five are **synthesized secondary research**: original paraphrase/summary written after cross-referencing multiple independent published translations and commentaries, not verbatim scripture translation, and not a citable primary source in their own right. They are Tier 5/6 (academic-adjacent synthesis and modern editorial research), useful for production planning, narrative detail, and risk-flagging, but any specific scriptural claim, name, or number drawn from them must still be checked against a Tier 1/2 source (a chosen canonical Vietnamese translation edition) before being quoted or presented as doctrinally final. No file contains verbatim quotations longer than approximately 15 words from any translation.
