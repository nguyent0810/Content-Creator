## Numbering Rule

`EP0XX` under `PRODUCTION_PACKAGES/KINH_DIA_TANG/` must equal the `Ep` column above. If an episode's real content does not match its assigned number in this table, the package must be renamed (folder + package_id + episode_id + asset_id + all internal references) before proceeding — do not leave a mismatched package in place and do not renumber this table to match an already-produced mismatch.

## Cross-Episode Continuity Notes

- EP001 already covers name/symbol/vow introduction at a high level. EP002 and EP003 go deeper into the same territory (etymology of "Địa Tạng", the vow text itself) — when producing EP002/EP003, read EP001's `03_AUDIO_SCRIPT_MASTER.md` first and avoid re-explaining what EP001 already established; each episode should add new depth, not repeat the introduction.
- EP004 already covers the Trayastrimsa assembly frame and filial piety as the sutra's opening context. EP008 revisits filial piety at a deeper, more applied level — avoid duplicating EP004's specific framing.
- EP005 ("Nghiệp duyên chúng sinh") should not re-teach karma mechanics in depth — that is EP009's job ("Tội, phước và nhân quả"). EP005's job is narrower: why beings suffer, owe, meet, and part (the relational/karmic-bond framing), not a general karma primer.
