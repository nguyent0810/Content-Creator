## Document Role

This is the central anti-duplication and anti-contradiction registry for the Kinh Địa Tạng Bồ Tát Bổn Nguyện production line. It exists because five parallel research agents (2026 research pass) independently found overlapping and sometimes conflicting facts, one already-shipped episode (EP001) turned out to contain a factual error inherited from an internal source file, and future episodes will keep needing the same disambiguations unless they are recorded once, centrally, here.

This file does not replace KP_BUD_001, CB_BUD_001/002/003, or SOURCE_REGISTRY.md — it is a cross-cutting index that points to and summarizes the load-bearing facts from those documents, specifically the facts that have already caused (or nearly caused) production errors, plus a running log of what narrative material each episode has already used, so repetition and confusion are visible at a glance.

Owner: BUD domain. Status: active. Every research brief, character usage plan, and episode planner in this production line should treat this file as a required read, alongside KP_BUD_001 and the relevant Character Bible(s).

## Lỗi nguồn đã phát hiện và đã sửa (Source errors found and corrected)

### Error 1: "Mục Kiền Liên Thánh" is not a name in this sutra

- **Lỗi gì:** An internal source file, `kinh-dia-tang-1.txt` (line 43), names a past-life figure of Ksitigarbha as "Mục Kiền Liên Thánh." This name does not appear anywhere in Kinh Địa Tạng Bồ Tát Bổn Nguyện, in any of the 13 chapters, in any translation checked.
- **Phát hiện ở đâu/khi nào:** Found during the 2026 parallel research pass, specifically documented in `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH03_04_13.md` ("Xác minh tên nhân vật chương 4"), and flagged as a known risk in the EP001 research brief (`DOMAINS/BUDDHISM/PRODUCTION_PACKAGES/KINH_DIA_TANG/EP001/_PRODUCTION/Long/_INTERNAL/01_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`, Risk Flags table) before this registry existed.
- **Kết luận đúng:** The name is a documented error, most likely arising from conflating two separate, unrelated elements: (a) **Mục Kiền Liên** (Maudgalyayana), a historical disciple of the Buddha known for the *Ullambana* (Vu Lan) narrative — an entirely different sutra — and (b) the character "Mục" (目, "eye") which legitimately appears in this sutra in two unrelated places: the name **Quang Mục** (光目, Chapter 4's protagonist) and the epithet **Liên Hoa Mục** (蓮華目, part of the Buddha-name "Thanh Tịnh Liên Hoa Mục Như Lai," also in Chapter 4). Do not use "Mục Kiền Liên Thánh" anywhere in any script, thumbnail, or narration. When a past-life figure must be named, use either "Thánh Nữ" (Chapter 1) or "Quang Mục" (Chapter 4) with the correct chapter attached — see Error 2.
- **Nguồn xác minh:** `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH03_04_13.md`; cross-checked against YMBA and wisdomlib.org English translations of Chapters 1 and 4; cross-checked against Vietnamese Buddhism explainer sites (vnctongiao.org, phatgiao.org.vn, gotoanthang.vn) that separately confirm Mục Kiền Liên and Địa Tạng Bồ Tát are distinct figures.

### Error 2: The "Brahman maiden" story belongs to Chapter 1, not Chapter 4

- **Lỗi gì:** An earlier task brief (predating this registry) described "Chapter 4" as containing "the Brahman-girl story." This is incorrect. The Brahman-maiden/Thánh Nữ story (mother Duyệt Đế Lợi, Buddha invoked = Giác Hoa Định Tự Tại Vương Như Lai, ghost king guide = Vô Độc) belongs to **Chapter 1**. Chapter 4 instead contains a different past-life story with a different protagonist: **Quang Mục** (mother unnamed, Buddha invoked = Thanh Tịnh Liên Hoa Mục Như Lai).
- **Phát hiện ở đâu/khi nào:** Found and explicitly documented during the 2026 parallel research pass, `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH03_04_13.md` ("Xác minh tên nhân vật chương 4"), which cross-checked both stories against a minimum of three independent sources each (two English translations of different lineage, plus Vietnamese-language sources).
- **Kết luận đúng:** Two distinct, textually separate stories exist and must never be merged or attributed to the wrong chapter:
  - **Chapter 1** — "Thánh Nữ" (Sacred/Holy Maiden), daughter of Brahman lineage. Mother: **Duyệt Đế Lợi**. Buddha invoked: **Giác Hoa Định Tự Tại Vương Như Lai**. Guide figure: ghost king **Vô Độc**. Outcome: mother already released from Avici hell and reborn in heaven by the time Thánh Nữ learns of her fate; Thánh Nữ vows to save all suffering beings.
  - **Chapter 4** — "Quang Mục" ("Bright Eyes"). Mother: unnamed in sources reviewed, condemned for killing fish/turtles (and their eggs) at scale, plus a speech-based offense. Buddha invoked: **Thanh Tịnh Liên Hoa Mục Như Lai**. Guide figure: an unnamed arhat, later identified as the past life of **Vô Tận Ý Bồ Tát** (Akshayamati). Outcome: mother reborn as a servant's child, warned she will die at 13 and relapse unless helped further; Quang Mục vows to save all beings in the three lower paths for countless kalpas before seeking her own enlightenment; her mother is confirmed as the past life of **Giải Thoát Bồ Tát**.
  - Both stories independently follow the same underlying pattern (grief for a mother's fate widening into a universal vow) — this repetition is a deliberate narrative structure in the sutra itself, not a duplication error. Scripts may note the parallel explicitly, but must keep the two protagonists, mothers, and invoked Buddha names distinct.
- **Nguồn xác minh:** `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH03_04_13.md`, cross-referencing YMBA and wisdomlib.org (Chapters 1 and 4, both from at least two independent translation lineages) plus bachhac.net (Vietnamese).

### Error 3: The ghost king Chủ Mạng, not King Yama (Diêm La Vương), is confirmed as a bodhisattva's manifestation in Chapter 8

- **Lỗi gì:** Risk of conflating two separate figures who both appear in Chapter 8 — King Yama (Diêm La Vương), who kneels, questions, and praises Ksitigarbha, and the ghost king **Chủ Mạng** ("Lord of Lifespans"), who tracks conception, birth, and lifelong conduct. Only one of these two receives explicit confirmation from the Buddha as a bodhisattva's compassionate manifestation, destined for future Buddhahood.
- **Phát hiện ở đâu/khi nào:** Documented during the 2026 parallel research pass in `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH07_08_09.md` (Chapter 8 section), flagged explicitly as the task's own named concern to avoid ("đừng nhầm 2 vị này") and reconfirmed in that draft's "Điểm cần con người xác minh thêm" section.
- **Kết luận đúng:** The Buddha confirms specifically that **Chủ Mạng** is the compassionate manifestation of a great bodhisattva, who will become a Buddha named **Vô Tướng Như Lai** after 170 more kalpas. **King Yama (Diêm La Vương) receives no such confirmation anywhere in this sutra.** His documented role in Chapter 8 is limited to kneeling, praising Ksitigarbha's power, and asking why rescued beings relapse into wrongdoing. Any script, thumbnail, or narration line that says or implies "Diêm La Vương is secretly a bodhisattva" is incorrect and must be corrected to refer to Chủ Mạng instead. See `CB_BUD_002_Diem_La_Vuong.md` for the full disambiguation.
- **Nguồn xác minh:** `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH07_08_09.md`, cross-referencing YMBA, wisdomlib.org (two translation editions), Vạn Phật Thánh (Hsuan Hua commentary tradition, Vietnamese), and bachhac.net (Vietnamese, specifically for the Chủ Mạng vow content).

### Note (not an error, but a boundary to preserve): Chapter 12 does not contain the newborn/servant-status/female-rebirth material

- **Lỗi gì (near-miss, not yet an actual production error):** These three topics (praying for a newborn/unborn child's protection; a servant or person of low social standing seeking better rebirth through devotion; a woman wishing to avoid female rebirth) could plausibly be assumed to belong to Chapter 12 ("Benefits from Seeing and Hearing") given its similar list-of-benefits structure, but cross-checked research confirms they do not appear there.
- **Phát hiện ở đâu/khi nào:** `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH10_11_12.md`, Chapter 12 section, cross-checked against two independent sources including Hsuan Hua's two-part commentary on Chapter 12 specifically to rule this in or out.
- **Kết luận đúng:** The newborn/pregnancy material and the "thoát thân nữ" (avoiding female rebirth) material belong to **Chapter 6**. The "người thuộc hạ tiện, nô bộc" (low social standing) material also belongs to **Chapter 6**. A parallel servant/social-standing theme also appears in **Chapter 13**'s benefit lists. Do not import any of this content into a Chapter 12 script.
- **Nguồn xác minh:** `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH10_11_12.md` and `RESEARCH_DRAFT_CH02_05_06.md` (Chapter 6 section).

## Thuật ngữ chuẩn hóa (Standardized terminology)

Use the left-hand form consistently across all scripts, titles, thumbnails, and metadata unless the right-hand column's exception condition applies.

| Standard term | Use for | Avoid / exception |
|---|---|---|
| **Địa Tạng Bồ Tát** | Default formal reference to the sutra's central figure in narration, titles, and doctrinal statements. | "Địa Tạng Vương Bồ Tát" / "Địa Tạng Vương" is an accepted common devotional/folk honorific form (per CB_BUD_001) and may be used in devotional-register or folk-context lines, but must never be allowed to imply worldly kingship or identity with Diêm La Vương. Default back to "Địa Tạng Bồ Tát" for formal/doctrinal statements. |
| **Diêm La Vương** | The chapter-8 figure who questions and praises Ksitigarbha. | Never use as a synonym for Địa Tạng Bồ Tát. Never use to name the bodhisattva-manifestation figure of Chapter 8 (that is Chủ Mạng). See CB_BUD_002. |
| **Chủ Mạng** | The ghost king in Chapter 8 confirmed as a bodhisattva's manifestation. | Do not merge with Diêm La Vương. Do not call him "Diêm La Vương's assistant" or similar subordinating phrase not supported by the text. |
| **Kiên Lao Địa Thần** | The named guardian earth-spirit character of Chapter 11. | Do not use "Địa Thần Hộ Pháp" as if it were his personal name — that is the chapter title. Do not merge with folk Thổ Địa/Thổ Công worship without an explicit "separate folk tradition" label. See CB_BUD_003. |
| **Thánh Nữ** | The Chapter 1 past-life protagonist (Brahman maiden). | Do not call her "Quang Mục." Do not attach her to Chapter 4. |
| **Quang Mục** | The Chapter 4 past-life protagonist ("Bright Eyes"). | Do not call her "Thánh Nữ." Do not attach her to Chapter 1. Never use "Mục Kiền Liên Thánh" for this or any other figure — see Error 1. |
| **kinh-dia-tang-1/2/3.txt** | Internal modern narration/reflection scripts (Tier 6, restricted, per SOURCE_REGISTRY.md). | Do not cite as a scriptural translation or as a source for specific chapter content, character names, or numeric claims. Usable only for narration-voice reference and the (unverified-as-verbatim) traditional vow-line wording. |
| **"Địa ngục vị không, thệ bất thành Phật. Chúng sinh độ tận, phương chứng Bồ Đề"** | The traditional sixteen-character vow formulation, when narrated. | Present as "a traditional formulation long associated with Địa Tạng's vow in devotional reception," never inside hard quotation marks as a verified verbatim scripture quotation (per EP001 precedent, reconfirmed by the 2026 research pass). |
| **Kinh Địa Tạng Bồ Tát Bổn Nguyện** | Full formal sutra title on first reference in an episode. | "Kinh Địa Tạng" is an accepted short form for repeated use within an episode. |

## Thông số sản xuất đã hiệu chỉnh (Calibrated production parameters)

### Narration pace: ~260 words/minute at default (1x) TTS speed

- **Discovered:** 2026-07-17, after the user rendered EP005's Long-form script (3903 words) through their actual TTS tool at default speed and reported a runtime of approximately 15 minutes.
- **Calculation:** 3903 words / 15 minutes ≈ 260 words/minute. This is roughly double the ~140-150 wpm figure used when planning EP001 and EP005 (an assumption carried over from generic contemplative-narration guidance, never actually measured against this project's real TTS output).
- **Conflicting data point (unresolved, noted for transparency):** EP004's manifest (`EP004/_INTERNAL/manifest.json`) records `word_count: 5222` and `video_prompt_total_audio_duration_seconds: 2410.154` (≈40.2 minutes), implying only ~130 wpm. This EP004 duration figure comes from the pre-2026-research-pass video-prompt pipeline, which `VIDEO_CREATION_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md` already flagged as self-certifying and partly unreliable — it may not reflect an actual completed voice render. The user confirmed the EP005 measurement came from an actual TTS render at default (1x) speed with a mainstream tool (Google/ElevenLabs/VBee-class), which is the higher-confidence data point.
- **Working standard going forward:** use **260 words/minute** to convert a target runtime into a target word count for both Long-form scripts and Shorts, until a future episode's real render provides another data point to confirm or revise this figure. Record any new measurement here.
- **Effect on existing episodes:** EP001 (4249 words ≈ 16.3 min) and EP005 (3903 words ≈ 15.0 min) are short relative to a 25-45 minute long-form target under this corrected pace. They were produced under the old (incorrect) pace assumption and were not retroactively lengthened — flagged here so a future editorial decision can be made about whether to extend them.
- **Effect on Shorts:** the word-count ranges in `CORE_OS/SHORTS_ENGINE.md`'s Universal Short Anatomy section were also written under the old ~150 wpm assumption and likely undershoot their stated second-count targets at the real 260 wpm pace. Revise if a Shorts batch is produced again and the mismatch matters for the use case.

## Cách cập nhật file này (How to update this file)

1. Before starting research for a new episode, read this entire file.
2. After the episode's script is finalized (not just drafted), add or update a row in "Câu chuyện/motif đã dùng theo tập" naming the specific story/chapter/motif used.
3. If the episode uses a recurring image already listed in "Ẩn dụ/hình ảnh đã dùng nhiều," update its usage count/list; if it introduces a new recurring image likely to be reused, add a new row.
4. If research for the new episode uncovers a new naming error, source conflict, or chapter-attribution mistake (in an internal file, a prior episode, or a previously-approved document), add a new numbered entry to "Lỗi nguồn đã phát hiện và đã sửa" following the existing format: Lỗi gì / Phát hiện ở đâu khi nào / Kết luận đúng / Nguồn xác minh.
5. Do not delete or silently overwrite prior entries in this file; if a later correction supersedes an earlier one, add a new entry that explicitly references and supersedes the old one, so the correction history remains visible.
