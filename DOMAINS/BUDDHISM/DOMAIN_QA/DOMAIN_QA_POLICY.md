# Buddhism Domain QA Policy

Required checks: doctrinal accuracy, school attribution, source citation, superstition avoidance, miracle-claim avoidance, karma no-victim-blame, grief safety, filial piety boundary safety, hell realm non-graphic framing, devotional humility, rebirth uncertainty, medical and mental health boundaries.

Escalate to HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED for suicide, abuse, illness cure claims, specific deceased rebirth claims, unclear scripture attribution, or conflicting tradition interpretations.
